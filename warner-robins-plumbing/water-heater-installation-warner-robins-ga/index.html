    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Warner Robins

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Water heater replacements, whole-house repipes, new fixtures in new locations, and sewer line work usually require a permit and a final inspection. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Warner Robins. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Warner Robins, GA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.
