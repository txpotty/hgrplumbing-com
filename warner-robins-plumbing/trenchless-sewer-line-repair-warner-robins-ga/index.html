    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Warner Robins

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Warner Robins

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Warner Robins, GA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Warner Robins, GA

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.
