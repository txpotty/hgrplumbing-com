    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Warner Robins, GA

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Warner Robins, GA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Warner Robins

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Warner Robins, GA

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Warner Robins

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Warner Robins, GA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Warner Robins

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Warner Robins

On twin-tank and progressive-flow units, we spend extra time on the meter and the tank switching. We service them every one to three months, depending on volume.
