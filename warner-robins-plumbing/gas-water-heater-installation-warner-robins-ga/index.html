    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Warner Robins, GA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Warner Robins, GA

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. Undersized vents spill CO. We test it every time.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Warner Robins, GA

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. HGR Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. We connect the gas, set the flue and check the Guardian flammable vapor sensor and the combustion air path so the safety features work as designed. Draft is checked last.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Warner Robins

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Warner Robins, GA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Testing comes last. Value and reliability are why people choose them.
