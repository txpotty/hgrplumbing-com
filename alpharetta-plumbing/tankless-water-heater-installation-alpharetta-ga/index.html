    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Alpharetta, GA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it.     Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first.
