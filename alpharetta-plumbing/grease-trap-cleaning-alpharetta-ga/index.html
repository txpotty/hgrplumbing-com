    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Alpharetta, GA

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Alpharetta

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. A hard grease cap gets broken up with a pressure wand before pumping, otherwise it plugs the suction hose and half of it stays in the tank. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Alpharetta, GA

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.

    Grease Line Jetting in Alpharetta

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. A camera confirms it afterward.
