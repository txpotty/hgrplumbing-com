    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more thorough cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    A plunger or a small hand auger can handle a simple hair clog near the drain. For deep clogs, main line backups or repeat problems, a pro has the machines and camera to find and fix the real cause.

    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs usually take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also rarely clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is safer and more effective.

    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old Alpharetta properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to check pipe condition and use the safest method.

    Tree roots are attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing significant blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.
