    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Alpharetta

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Alpharetta

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Alpharetta, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Alpharetta, GA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
