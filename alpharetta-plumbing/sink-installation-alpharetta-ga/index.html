    Pedestal Sink Installation in Alpharetta, GA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Alpharetta

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Alpharetta, GA

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Alpharetta, GA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Alpharetta

The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
