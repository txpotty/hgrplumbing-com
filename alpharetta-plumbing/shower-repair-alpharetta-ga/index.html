    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Alpharetta

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Alpharetta

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Alpharetta, GA

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. Vandal-resistant heads are available. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Alpharetta, GA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Alpharetta, GA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Alpharetta, GA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Alpharetta

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action.
