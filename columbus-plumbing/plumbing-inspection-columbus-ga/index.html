    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Columbus, GA

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Columbus, GA

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Columbus, GA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Water Heater Inspections in Columbus

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Columbus, GA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Columbus

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Columbus

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Columbus

    Businesses in Columbus use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. Backflow devices, heaters, flush valves, floor drains, trap primers and the sewer all get checked. Downtime costs money. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Columbus, GA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.
