    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Columbus, GA

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Columbus, GA

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will usually show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the sensible move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.
