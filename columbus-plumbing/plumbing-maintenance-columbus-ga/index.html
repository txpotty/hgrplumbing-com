    Sump Pump Maintenance in Columbus, GA

    Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain in a short time, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Columbus

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Columbus, GA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Columbus

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Gas work is done to code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Repipe Assessments in Columbus, GA

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. We quote PEX and copper. Work can be phased.
