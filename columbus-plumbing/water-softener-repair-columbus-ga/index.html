    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Columbus, GA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports.     Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.
