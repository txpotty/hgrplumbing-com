    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Columbus, GA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Columbus, GA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Columbus

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Columbus

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Columbus

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Columbus

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Columbus, GA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Columbus, GA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.
