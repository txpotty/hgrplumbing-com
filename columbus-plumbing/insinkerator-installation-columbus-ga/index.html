    Hard Water and Disposals in Columbus

    Private wells still serve a sizable share of rural Georgia homes, and iron, sediment, and low pH can shorten the life of pumps, tanks, and copper lines. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. A scaled unit gets loud. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Columbus

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Columbus

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Columbus

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. First, the sink gets a disposal flange. The tailpiece and trap have to be reworked, and the cabinet needs a switched outlet, which means an electrician if there is no power under the sink already. We also check the drain slope to the stack.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Columbus, GA

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. We take them with us. The old cord, any replaced mount ring and the rusty slip nuts go too, so you are not stuck with a bag of parts on trash day. Most replacements finish in one visit.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Columbus

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone.
