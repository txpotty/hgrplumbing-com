    Control Valve and Injector Service in Columbus, GA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Columbus

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we analyze the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to steady soft water throughout your home in Columbus.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Columbus

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Columbus.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Columbus, GA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Columbus

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain plainly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Columbus, GA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Columbus, GA

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to ensure these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Columbus, GA.

    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Columbus

    Our routine tune-ups involve a complete inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and confirm the resin bed is working effectively. This preventative service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.
