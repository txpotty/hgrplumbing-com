    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Columbus

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under floors and slabs. Snaking clears a path, but the grease soon builds back. Jetting with a rotating nozzle peels that layer off and flushes it downstream. Homeowners who cook often and restaurants benefit the most from regular kitchen line jetting.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Columbus, GA

    Hard water can lead to significant mineral scale buildup inside pipes over time, reducing flow and creating rough surfaces where other debris can snag. Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so more mineral-heavy water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. Our hydrojet plumbing service is very effective at blasting away stubborn calcium and magnesium scale. By restoring the inner diameter of your pipes, we improve drainage and prevent future clogs caused by mineral accumulation. This is particularly important for homes with aging galvanized or cast iron pipes in Columbus.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Columbus, GA
