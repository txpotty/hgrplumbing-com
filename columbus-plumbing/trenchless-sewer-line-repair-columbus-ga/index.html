    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Columbus, GA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Georgia homeowners.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Columbus

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Columbus, GA

    Sewer lines in Columbus, GA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Much of Georgia sits on dense red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Columbus, GA

    While the upfront cost of trenchless sewer repair in Columbus, GA can sometimes be comparable to or slightly higher than traditional methods, the overall savings are often significant. This is because trenchless repairs eliminate the need for extensive excavation, backfilling and landscape restoration. Homeowners save money on repairing driveways, patios and gardens, making it a more sensible choice in the long run for many.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Columbus, GA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Columbus

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Columbus that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Columbus, GA

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be applied to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Columbus, GA. This preserves your home's interior and reduces renovation costs.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Columbus, GA

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap.
