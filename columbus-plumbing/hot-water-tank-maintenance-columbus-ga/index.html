    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Columbus

    The cost for hot water tank maintenance in Columbus, GA usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required like anode rod replacement. We provide a detailed quote before any work begins.

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service generally takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part swaps like an anode rod.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining begins to rust.

    HGR Plumbing stands behind our work. While maintenance services usually don't have long-term warranties like new installations, we guarantee our workmanship for a set period. Any parts replaced during maintenance will carry their manufacturer's warranty.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can deny claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records safeguards your warranty.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, strange noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These indicate issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to inefficient operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service heads off these costly and dangerous issues.
