    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Columbus

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.

    PEX Repiping in Columbus

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Columbus

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, and keep water on at night whenever we can. Then patching.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Columbus, GA

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Columbus

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Columbus

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Columbus, GA

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.
