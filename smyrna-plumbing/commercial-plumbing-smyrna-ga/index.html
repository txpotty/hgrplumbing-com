    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Smyrna, GA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Smyrna

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Smyrna

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Much of Georgia sits on dense red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.
