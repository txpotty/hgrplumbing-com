    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Smyrna, GA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Smyrna, GA

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. Commercial units run 1 to 5 HP, usually on a dedicated circuit, and many need a water solenoid and a control panel mounted on the wall.     Standard InSinkErator Installation in Smyrna

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Smyrna, GA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Smyrna

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.
