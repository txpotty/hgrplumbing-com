    Picking the Right Softener for Smyrna, GA Water

    Softener Loop Installation in Smyrna, GA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Smyrna, GA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. Outdoor hose bibs usually stay hard. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Smyrna

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Smyrna, GA

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. For a small house on moderately hard water, a basic metered single-tank unit often does the job for much less than a twin-tank system. You see the choices on one page.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Smyrna

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Smyrna, GA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Smyrna

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Smyrna

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.
