    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Smyrna

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. We replace any tripped thermal switch.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Smyrna, GA

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. You get the old valve back if you want it.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Smyrna

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Smyrna

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column.     Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Smyrna

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve.
