    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Smyrna, GA

    Power vent water heaters use a fan to push exhaust through PVC or other approved vent pipe. This allows installation in places without a chimney or vertical flue. Power vent units need an electrical outlet nearby. We install the vent run to manufacturer specs and check for proper termination. These units offer flexible placement options.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Smyrna

    Atmospheric vent water heaters rely on natural draft to move exhaust through a metal flue. They are common in older homes and are often the most budget-friendly gas option. We inspect the existing vent for damage, proper slope and clearance. If the vent is undersized or corroded, we replace it. Proper draft is critical for safety.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Smyrna

    Direct vent water heaters draw combustion air from outside and vent exhaust through a sealed system. They are a good choice for tight utility rooms or interior closets. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall with proper clearances. Many direct vent models do not need electricity. They offer safe, efficient operation.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Smyrna

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 and 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or major gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work quickly to minimize disruption to your home.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced annually. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, ensuring safe operation.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years. We also warranty our installation labor.
