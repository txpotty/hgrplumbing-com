    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Augusta, GA

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. For basement baths and guest suites below the sewer, we also install ejector systems with a sealed basin, check valve and vent.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Augusta, GA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Softener Loop and Conditioner Installs in Augusta

    Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters.     Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Augusta

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector.
