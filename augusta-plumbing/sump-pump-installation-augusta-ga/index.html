    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Augusta

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Augusta, GA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
