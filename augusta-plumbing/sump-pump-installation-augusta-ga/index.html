    Sump Pump Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Augusta, GA

    A pump replacement in an existing pit typically costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the cost but provide extra protection. We give you a written quote that explains each option. That way you can choose the right level of protection for your budget.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Augusta

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are complex plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without sacrificing quality.

    HGR Plumbing installs a variety of reputable sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your particular needs and budget for your Augusta home.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Augusta basement.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is usually required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This ensures the work meets local building and plumbing codes. HGR Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Augusta, GA to ensure your installation is fully compliant.

    A simple replacement often does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or lots that collect storm runoff can benefit a lot. A sump pump protects against fast-rising storm flooding.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned once a year, especially before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to confirm it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent unexpected failures.
