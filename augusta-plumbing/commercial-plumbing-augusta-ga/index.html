    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Augusta, GA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Augusta, GA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Running toilets count too. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Augusta, GA

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space.     Grease Trap Installation in Augusta

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Augusta

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. We tag every passing device. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Augusta, GA

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Many 6-inch laterals can be lined in a single day without cutting the parking lot or closing a door.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Augusta, GA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.
