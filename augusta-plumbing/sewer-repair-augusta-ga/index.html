    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Augusta

    Before any repair, we use high-resolution video camera inspection to accurately locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This non-invasive method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a clear diagnosis and repair plan. It's crucial for understanding issues in older homes in Augusta and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Augusta

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Augusta

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Georgia homes.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Augusta

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Georgia, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining solutions to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Augusta

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Augusta dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Augusta

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Georgia properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Augusta.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Augusta, GA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Augusta

    The cost of sewer repair in Augusta, GA can vary widely, typically ranging from $300 for a simple clog removal to $6,000 or more for extensive line replacement using trenchless methods. Factors include the extent of damage, pipe material, accessibility and whether excavation is needed. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    A small rental auger can clear a soft clog, but it will not fix a crack, belly or root intrusion. Forcing a cable through a broken pipe can make the damage worse. A camera inspection tells you whether you have a clog or a structural problem.

    Roots usually enter through existing gaps at joints, then expand as they grow. Over time they can crack clay pipe, separate joints and create a full blockage. Cutting roots helps short-term, but sealing or replacing the joint is the long-term fix.

    Yes, weather can affect sewer lines. Around Augusta, sticky air means sweating cold-water pipes and toilet tanks are common, and that moisture can quietly rot subfloors if nobody notices. Heavy rains can saturate the ground, putting pressure on pipes and widening existing cracks. Dry spells can cause soil to shift, leading to pipe misalignment or breaks.

    The length of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Trenchless repairs like pipe lining can often be completed in one to two days. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method that allows for pipe repair or replacement without extensive digging. Techniques like pipe lining, also called cured-in-place pipe, or pipe bursting minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping. It's an excellent option for preserving your property while fixing underground pipes.
