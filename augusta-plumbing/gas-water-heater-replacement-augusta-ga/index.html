    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Augusta, GA

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Augusta, GA

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Augusta

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. Model choice follows your hot water use. The install meets current code.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Augusta

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Augusta, GA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Augusta

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Georgia plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Augusta

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors.
