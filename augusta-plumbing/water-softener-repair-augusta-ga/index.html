    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Augusta, GA

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and occasional repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. HGR Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these specialized units in Augusta, GA, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Augusta

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. HGR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, properly removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Augusta.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Augusta

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process revitalizes your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Augusta

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Augusta

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Augusta

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.
