    Finding and Repairing Foundation Leaks in Augusta, GA

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Augusta, GA

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Standing water under a house rots sills and joists and grows mold on the subfloor, so we repair the source and look at the vapor barrier while we are down there. A sump pump may make sense in a Augusta, GA crawl space that floods from outside.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Augusta

    Slab leaks show up quietly. Sometimes all you get is a warm spot on the tile or a bill that creeps up. HGR Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides, then uses acoustic and infrared gear to locate the break under your concrete. For Augusta homes, the fix might be a spot repair through the floor, a reroute through the attic, or a tunnel from outside, depending on where the leak sits and how old the pipe is.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Augusta, GA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we explain each one.

    Pricing Foundation Leak Repairs in Augusta, GA

    Price depends on where the leak sits and what is on top of it. Tile costs more to open. Once the leak is found, you get one written price for a spot repair and one for a reroute, patching included, so the comparison is fair. That holds for every Augusta, GA job.

    Commercial Foundation Leak Services in Augusta

    Shops and offices in Augusta have longer runs and more fixtures on the slab. That makes locating slower. Locating and testing can happen after closing or on a weekend, and the concrete cutting gets booked into your slowest hours. HGR Plumbing sketches the pipe route for your facilities file.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Augusta, GA

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. That's the main tool. HGR Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, which narrows the spot to a foot or two in most houses we test, in Augusta, GA or anywhere else.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Augusta

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. HGR Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Augusta foundation is sound again.

    Leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. The repair is a separate price.     A spot repair is often done in a few hours. Tunneling adds time. A reroute through the attic or a tunnel dug from outside can take one to three days, depending on how far the line runs and how much framing or soil is in the way. Your Augusta tech will give you a closer estimate.

    A water bill that jumps for no reason is often the first sign. Watch for warm spots on tile. Musty smells near the foundation in Augusta are another red flag.

    Crack sealing can be done in a few hours. Slab work takes days.     Spot repairs often skip the permit, while reroutes and repipes may need one depending on the city. We file for permits whenever required.

    We repair copper, PEX and PVC lines in Augusta homes. For PEX, that includes Uponor expansion fittings and Viega crimp systems, and we match the fitting type already in your house so the repair is compatible with the rest of the plumbing. Code-approved parts only.

    Larger plumbing repairs under a foundation in Augusta, GA usually need a permit. Water heater replacements, repiping, new fixtures in new locations, and sewer line work usually require a permit and a final inspection. We handle the filing and the inspection.
