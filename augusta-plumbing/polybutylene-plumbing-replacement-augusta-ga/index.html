    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Augusta

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Augusta, GA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard. We call 811 first. Then we trench the route, install new PEX or copper service pipe and connect it to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Copper Replacement Option in Augusta

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install, so we help you weigh the cost, your water conditions and how long you plan to stay in the house.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Augusta

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time. When a poly line leaks, we cut out the bad section and splice in PEX with approved transition fittings, which stops the leak and gives you time to schedule a full replacement on your own terms.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Augusta

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Sometimes it has to wait. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. We will be honest about what is left.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Augusta

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. If the meter's leak dial moves with everything off, we track the leak down with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging instead of guessing. Finding it lets us make a temporary repair and plan the full replacement.
