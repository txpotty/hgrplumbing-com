    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Augusta, GA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Augusta

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Augusta residence.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Augusta, GA

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Augusta, GA where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Augusta

    Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain in a short time, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Augusta, GA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Hard freezes are occasional in Georgia, but when a cold snap arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes in crawlspaces, attics, and exterior walls.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Augusta

    HGR Plumbing is proficient in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability.     Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Augusta, GA

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Augusta, GA.
