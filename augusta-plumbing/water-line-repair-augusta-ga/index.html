    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Augusta

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Augusta is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Augusta

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Augusta, GA

We handle all required permits and inspections.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Augusta, GA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until serious damage has occurred. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Augusta, minimizing the need for extensive excavation.     Trenchless water line repair is a method of repairing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging.     Most standard water line repairs can be completed within a few hours to a full day, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Regular plumbing inspections can also help identify potential problems before they escalate. For older homes in Augusta, proactive pipe replacement might be the best long-term solution.
