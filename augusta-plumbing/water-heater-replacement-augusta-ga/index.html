    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Augusta

    Electric water heaters in Augusta usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Augusta, GA

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Leaks only get worse.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Augusta, GA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Augusta, GA

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Augusta, GA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.
