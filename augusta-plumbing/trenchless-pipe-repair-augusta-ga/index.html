    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Augusta

    Older Augusta homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Augusta

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines.     Pipe Bursting in Augusta, GA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.
