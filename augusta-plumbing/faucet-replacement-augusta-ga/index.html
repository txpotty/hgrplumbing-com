    Moen Faucet Replacement in Augusta

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. A stuck 1225 makes the handle stiff and the temperature wander, and after many years in place it can be cemented in by minerals. Sometimes pulling it cracks the body. That is when we replace the faucet.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Outdoor spigots wear out from sun, hoses and hard closing. Before a cold night, unhooking garden hoses, covering hose bibs, and letting a faucet drip are usually enough to prevent a burst line. We replace worn spigots with frost-free hose bibs where needed and add vacuum breakers for backflow protection, since code requires one on every hose connection. We also check inside the wall. A bib that leaked for months can leave wet insulation behind.

    Repair or Replace a Faucet in Augusta, GA

    Repair or replace comes down to math. A mid-range faucet costs roughly $150 to $400, installation adds about $150 to $300, and two or three cartridge repairs on an old faucet can add up to the same number. You see both prices. Old faucets with discontinued parts usually tip toward replacement.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which.
