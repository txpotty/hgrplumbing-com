    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Augusta

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    In many areas, permits may be required for significant plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. HGR Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Augusta, GA so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can signal pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    We install Grundfos, Goulds and Franklin Electric pumps. These brands are reliable and widely supported.

    New well pumps typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. HGR Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is highly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.

    Most well pumps last 8 to 15 years. Sediment, hard water and frequent cycling can cut lifespan.
