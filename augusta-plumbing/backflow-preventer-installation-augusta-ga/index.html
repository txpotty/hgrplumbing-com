    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Augusta, GA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Augusta, GA

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost a good deal more in parts and labor. Size, brand, enclosure needs and how much existing pipe must be modified all affect the price. Replacing a failed old device with a new one is usually cheaper than repeated rebuilds. We give you an itemized quote that includes the initial certification test.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Augusta

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Augusta

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Augusta. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HGR Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Augusta

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Augusta, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Augusta

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water utility.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Augusta, GA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Augusta, GA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.
