    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Augusta

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Augusta, GA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Augusta

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. Our plumber finds and fixes the leak, then runs the test your gas company requires before unlocking the meter. The truck carries what we need. We do not leave until the gauge holds steady and the system is ready for the utility.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Augusta

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Augusta

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Augusta, GA

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. Coastal and low-lying parts of Georgia face flooding risk during hurricane season, and saturated ground can push water into crawlspaces and basements.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Augusta, GA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Augusta, GA
