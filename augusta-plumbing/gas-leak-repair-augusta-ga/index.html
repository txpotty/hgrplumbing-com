    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Augusta

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. The first step is a pressure gauge on the piping, which tells us whether the system is actually losing gas. Then the sniffer comes out. Our combustible gas detector reads in parts per million, so we can follow the reading fitting by fitting until it peaks. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final confirmation.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Augusta

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. We check regulator lockup and vent lines along with the joints. Rooftop units leak too. Where downtime costs you money, we isolate only the affected branch and keep the rest of the building running. Staff get a short briefing on the shutoff locations.

    Gas Line Repair and Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a pressure test.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Augusta, GA

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. We work from the meter inward, testing each section as we go. Drywall stays intact when possible. Rerouting with CSST past a leak in a finished wall is often cheaper than cutting and patching drywall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Augusta, GA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Augusta

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. When the leak is small enough, we plan the shutdown for after close. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. Each hose and manifold joint gets tested, and we tag what we isolated so your staff knows what is off. New pipe still needs the commercial inspection.
