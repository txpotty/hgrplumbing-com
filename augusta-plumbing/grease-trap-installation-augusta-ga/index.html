    The cost of grease trap installation in Augusta, GA varies widely based on the size, type, and complexity of the system.     The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to extending its lifespan.

    Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours. Our experienced plumbers will conduct a complete assessment of your Augusta business to recommend the appropriate size and type of grease trap that meets local codes and your operational needs.

    An indoor under-sink trap can often be installed in a day. Outdoor interceptors typically take several days to a week or more, depending on excavation, concrete work and inspection scheduling.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.
