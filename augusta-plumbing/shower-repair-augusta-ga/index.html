    Shower Repairs We Handle in Augusta, GA

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Augusta, GA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Augusta

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Augusta

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Augusta, GA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Augusta

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Augusta, GA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Augusta

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Augusta, GA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.
