    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Augusta, GA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Augusta

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Augusta

    Many homes in Augusta have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Augusta, GA

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Augusta

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Attic and crawlspace temperatures climb fast in summer, which speeds up wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping run through those spaces. An encapsulated crawl space can significantly improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use tough, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.
