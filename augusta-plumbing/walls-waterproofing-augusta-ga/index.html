    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Augusta

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Augusta

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Augusta take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down.     Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Augusta, GA

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Augusta, GA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak.     Crawl Space Encapsulation in Augusta

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Attic and crawlspace temperatures climb fast in summer, which speeds up wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping run through those spaces. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Drainage System Integration in Augusta, GA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Augusta

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Augusta, GA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain.
