    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Augusta

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. Each visit includes a hardness test, logged for your warranty file. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Commercial Softener Service in Augusta

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set a service schedule around your hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Augusta, GA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Augusta

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Augusta, GA

    Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Morton and the store brands all use different valves, and parts for some of them only come through the dealer. We stock seal kits for the common ones. For a proprietary unit, we tell you up front if a part has to be ordered and how long it usually takes.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Augusta, GA

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Augusta

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a seal kit and a new piston, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. Cheaper than a new head.
