    New Construction Shower Installation in Augusta, GA

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. We rough in valves, supply lines and drains once the walls are framed, set each valve to the depth of the planned finish and locate the drain for the pan you picked. Then the rough-in inspection. Trim goes on once tile is done.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Augusta, GA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Augusta, GA

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. A cracked pan or a leaking valve means pulling the shower, checking the framing and getting a new unit in as soon as it is safe. Dry framing first. Then the new shower goes in.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Augusta, GA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Augusta, GA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Augusta

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.
