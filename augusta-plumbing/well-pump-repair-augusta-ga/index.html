    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Augusta

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. We test the motor from the surface first. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Same-Day Well Pump Repair in Augusta

    Hot weather makes a dry house worse. We respond fast to no-water calls and aim to restore service the same day. If a new pump has to be ordered, we can sometimes set up a temporary solution to get the house through. While the truck is on the way, the dispatcher can help you check the breaker and switch.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Augusta

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. HGR Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Augusta, GA well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Augusta, GA

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Augusta runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. HGR Plumbing prices both up front.     Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Augusta, GA

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Georgia electrical code before power goes back on.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Augusta

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Augusta, GA well.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Augusta

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Augusta

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.
