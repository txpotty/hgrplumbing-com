    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Augusta, GA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Augusta

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Augusta, GA

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. A small vent can backdraft. Draft gets tested on every job.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Augusta

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. We install their Defender models to the manual, keeping the bottom air intake clear and the clearances right. They are built heavy. Code-approved installs every time.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Augusta

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Augusta, GA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
