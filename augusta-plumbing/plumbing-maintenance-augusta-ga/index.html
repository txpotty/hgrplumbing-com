    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Augusta

    Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Augusta

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup.
