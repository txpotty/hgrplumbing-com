    Slab Leak Reroutes in Macon

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Macon

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach.     Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Macon

    The cost for professional slab leak detection in Macon, GA usually ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for diagnosis. This initial detection fee is separate from any repair costs, which can vary a lot based on the leak's location and required pipe repair.

    Some homeowner's insurance policies may cover the cost of accessing and repairing the slab leak, especially if the leak was sudden and accidental. However, coverage often varies, and damage caused by long-term neglect might not be covered. We recommend checking your specific policy details or contacting your insurance provider for details.

    Common signs of a slab leak include unusually high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also indicate moisture buildup.

    Slab leaks can cause significant damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and major, costly repairs.

    Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies a lot, so it's important to check your specific policy with your insurance provider.

    Once the slab leak is precisely detected, our technician will provide you with a detailed report and discuss the best repair options. These options may include trenchless repair, re-routing the affected pipe or traditional slab excavation and repair. We will then provide a clear estimate for the repair work.

    For major slab leak repairs, especially those involving excavation or extensive re-piping, a plumbing permit from the local building department is often required. HGR Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes so your repair is compliant with local codes and regulations, protecting your investment.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.
