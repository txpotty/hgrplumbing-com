    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Macon, GA

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Houses from the late seventies through the mid nineties may still have gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is known to fail and is worth replacing. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Tree Root Removal in Macon, GA Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Once inside, they grow fast. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Root Masses in Macon Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. We cut roots in several passes, then look on camera to judge the damage and talk about lining or a spot repair.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Macon, GA

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Macon

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Macon, GA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.
