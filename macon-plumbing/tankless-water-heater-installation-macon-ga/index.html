    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Macon

    Conversions in Macon usually take a full day. We drain and remove the tank, upsize and test the gas, run a new vent through a wall or roof, then mount, plumb and fire the unit with service valves. You shower that night. Inspection follows within days.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Macon, GA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Private wells still serve a large share of rural Georgia homes, and iron, sediment, and low pH can shorten the life of pumps, tanks, and copper lines. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Macon, GA

    Upfront, a tankless install in Macon, GA costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Less standby loss, a life of up to 20 years versus roughly 10 for a tank, and replaceable parts all count over the years. Ask for both prices.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Macon

    Gas tankless units in Macon are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.
