    Many manufacturers offer 10 to 15 year heat exchanger warranties and shorter coverage on parts. We also guarantee our installation labor.

    Yes, tankless water heaters are very energy efficient in almost any climate. Since they only heat water on demand, they avoid the standby heat loss common with traditional tanks, which can be significant even in milder seasons.
