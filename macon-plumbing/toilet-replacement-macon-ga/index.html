    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Macon, GA

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. We confirm the clearances before ordering. Sensor flush valves are an easy add during the swap, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Macon, GA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Macon, GA

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. A WaterSense model uses 1.28 gallons or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Check with your water provider about rebates.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Macon

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Macon, GA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Macon, GA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.
