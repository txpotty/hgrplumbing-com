    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Macon

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Macon

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Sectional Point Repair in Macon

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Macon laterals.

    Pipe Bursting in Macon, GA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Commercial Trenchless Solutions in Macon

    Commercial sites in Macon have longer runs and more laterals. Parking lots sit on top. Trenchless repair lets schools, offices and shops keep their lots and sidewalks open, since we work through cleanouts and manholes and only cut a small pit where one is needed. We plan work for slow periods.
