    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Macon

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Roots love those joints. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, then we cut them out and camera the line for damage. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.
