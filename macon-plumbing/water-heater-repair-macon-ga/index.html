    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Macon, GA

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips from the relief valve, drain valve or supply connections are repairable for a modest cost. A leak from the tank body or the bottom seam, however, usually means the inner lining has failed. We pinpoint the source, protect your floors and give you a straight repair or replace recommendation.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Macon, GA

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. A rotten egg smell from hot water usually goes away with the right anode choice.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Macon

    Popping, rumbling or crackling sounds usually mean a thick layer of sediment is trapping water under it and overheating the tank bottom. We drain and flush the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity, lowers energy use and helps extend tank life. We usually recommend yearly flushes for homes on hard water.
