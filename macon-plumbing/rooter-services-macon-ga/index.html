    Tree roots are naturally drawn to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a common issue in Macon neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Macon. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be needed. We will handle all required permitting if that situation arises.

Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.
