    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Macon, GA

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes keep most supply and drain repairs easier to reach. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Macon

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. You see puddles near the foundation. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and repair broken runs so downspout water gets carried away from the footing. The discharge point gets checked as well.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Macon, GA

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Drain Vent Repair in Macon, GA

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Preventive Drain Maintenance in Macon

    Scheduled cleaning keeps buildup from turning into a repair. Once a year is common. For older homes in Macon with cast iron or a history of kitchen clogs, a yearly jetting and camera check catches small defects before they back up. Scale builds fastest where water is hard.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Macon

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Hard water makes it worse. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Figure on about an hour for a typical fix.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Macon, GA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Macon

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe. We replace cracked tailpieces, fix slip nuts and correct trap height, and when grease has built up deep in the branch, we jet it clean and check the slope so it does not come right back. We look at the disposal outlet too.
