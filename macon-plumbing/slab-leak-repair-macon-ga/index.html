    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Macon, GA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Macon

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Macon with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Macon

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.
