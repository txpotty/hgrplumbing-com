    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Macon, GA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Macon, GA

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid.     Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Macon

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. We test both appliances at once.

    Evolution Series Installation in Macon

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Macon

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.
