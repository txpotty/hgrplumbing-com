    Fixing Showers in Macon, GA

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Macon

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Macon

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then call us. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Macon, GA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Macon

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Macon, GA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Macon

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Macon, GA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Macon

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.
