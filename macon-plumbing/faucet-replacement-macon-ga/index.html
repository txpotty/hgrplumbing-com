    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. The hose wears, the spray button sticks, and after a few years the docking magnet or weight stops pulling the head back into place. We swap the whole faucet. We clip the new hose weight at the right height and connect fresh braided supplies to both stops.

    Commercial Faucet Replacement in Macon

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Macon, GA

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle Delta faucets use a ball valve with seats and springs, while newer ones use a cartridge, and that difference decides whether a repair is worth doing. We identify the model on site. A sound body sometimes only needs parts.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Macon

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. The control box mounts on the cabinet wall above the floor, and it needs either a battery pack or an outlet the cord can reach, which some sink bases do not have. We check that first. Sensor range is set last.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Macon

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. Sometimes a new hose is enough. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Macon

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense bathroom faucets drop to 1.5 gallons per minute, and the aerated stream feels about the same in your hands. The savings show on the bill. A WaterSense lav faucet is an easy swap.

    Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Macon, GA

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. Lav faucet nuts hide behind the bowl in a tight gap, and after a couple of decades they tend to fuse to the threaded shank. A basin wrench or a cutter gets them off. The pop-up drain gets replaced too, because its old gasket has usually cracked by now.

    Urgent Faucet Replacement in Macon, GA

    Water that will not shut off at the faucet is an urgent call. Close the stop under the sink by turning it clockwise, and if the stop is seized or leaks at the stem, close the main. Urgent calls get priority. Our plumber shuts the water down, pulls the broken faucet and puts in a new one from the truck so you have a working sink again tonight.
