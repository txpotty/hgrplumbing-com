    PEX Pipe Replacement in Macon, GA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Macon

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Macon, GA

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast. Most emergency repipes still finish within the normal two to four day window once work starts.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Macon

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    PEX Repiping in Macon, GA

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Drain Line Repiping in Macon

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Macon

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Macon

    Most whole-home repipes in Macon, GA fall somewhere between $4,000 and $15,000. Bigger homes cost more. Square footage, fixture count, material choice and the amount of drywall work along the route set the final price. We quote it in writing.

    Most whole-home repipes take three to five days, including patching. Large houses may need more time. We turn the water off in the morning and back on before we leave each evening, so you can cook, shower and do dishes at night even while the job is still in progress.

    For copper, we use domestic Type L from mills such as Mueller and Cerro. Uponor PEX-A is our usual PEX. We also install Viega and Zurn PEX on some jobs, but we stick with one brand's pipe and fittings throughout a house so the manufacturer warranty stays valid.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX costs less. PEX bends, resists scale and handles freezes better, and copper is rigid, UV-tolerant and proven but pricier and vulnerable to aggressive water.

    Most families stay home the whole time. Water is off during work hours. Each evening the water comes back on, and we keep floors covered and clean up daily so the house stays livable.

    A repipe needs a permit, specialized tools, code knowledge and a passing pressure test. It takes skill and time. A DIY repipe can leave hidden leaks inside walls, fail inspection or void the pipe manufacturer's warranty, and any of those can cost more than hiring a licensed crew in the first place.

    A documented repipe removes a known risk that buyers and inspectors look for. Buyers notice it. Replacing polybutylene, Kitec or old galvanized makes a home easier to insure and easier to sell, and a permit record plus a transferable warranty gives the next owner proof the work was done.

    Most repipes in Macon, GA take three to five working days for an average house. Larger homes run longer. Copper takes a little longer than PEX because every turn needs a fitting, and the drywall patching usually adds a final day after the plumbing passes inspection.
