    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Johns Creek

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    Most standard gas water heater installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, more complex jobs, such as converting to a tankless unit or major gas line modifications, might take a full day.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced once a year. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, ensuring safe operation.

    New gas water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. HGR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, ensuring peace of mind.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, or visible leaks around the base of the unit. Older units, particularly those over 10 to 12 years old, are also candidates for replacement.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for gas water heater installation. We pull permits when required and make sure the work passes inspection.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    We install all major reputable brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State Water Heaters and Navien for tankless models. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your particular needs and budget.
