    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Johns Creek

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Johns Creek

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Johns Creek

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems. Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all kitchen faucet styles, including pull-down, pull-out, and commercial-style fixtures. We understand the importance of a fully functional kitchen sink for cooking and cleaning. We address everything from leaks around the base to issues with water filtration attachments.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Johns Creek

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Johns Creek bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Johns Creek, GA

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can lower your repair cost.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    If water keeps running from the tub spout while the shower is on, the diverter is worn. We replace diverter spouts, stems and gates and repair tub faucet drips. Slip-on and threaded spouts each install differently, so we bring the right type for your setup. This repair brings back full shower pressure.
