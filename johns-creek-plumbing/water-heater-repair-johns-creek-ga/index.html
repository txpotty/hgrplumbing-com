    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Johns Creek

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. We test each one, clean the burner and pilot assembly and check draft at the vent. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Johns Creek, GA

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. Hard water can eat through one in a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Johns Creek

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom.
