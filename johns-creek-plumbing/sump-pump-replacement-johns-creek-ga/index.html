    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Johns Creek

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Johns Creek, GA

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Johns Creek

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Johns Creek

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Johns Creek

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Johns Creek, GA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Johns Creek, GA

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Johns Creek

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.
