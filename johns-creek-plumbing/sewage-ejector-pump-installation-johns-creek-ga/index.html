    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Johns Creek

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Coastal and low-lying parts of Georgia face flooding risk during hurricane season, and saturated ground can push water into crawlspaces and basements. Battery backup is an option.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Johns Creek

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Johns Creek, GA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Johns Creek

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Their packaged systems come with the basin, lid, pump and check valve matched together, which cuts down on field fitting and gives the inspector a clean, listed assembly to look at. A full-port valve gets added.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Johns Creek, GA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Johns Creek

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes make most supply and drain repairs easier to reach. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Johns Creek, GA

    Businesses in Johns Creek, GA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house.
