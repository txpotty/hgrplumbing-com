    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Johns Creek

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Johns Creek, GA

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Johns Creek, GA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Johns Creek
