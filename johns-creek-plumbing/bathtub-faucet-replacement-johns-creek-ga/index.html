    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. Expect rusted galvanized supplies, valves with no stops, and steel spout nipples that snap. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Johns Creek

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Johns Creek

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it.     Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Johns Creek

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Johns Creek, GA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Johns Creek, GA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.
