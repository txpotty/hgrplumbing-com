    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Johns Creek

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Johns Creek home from unexpected flooding.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Johns Creek, GA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Johns Creek, GA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Johns Creek

    Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain fast, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Johns Creek, GA
