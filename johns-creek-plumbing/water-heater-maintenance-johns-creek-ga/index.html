    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Johns Creek

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Johns Creek, GA

    Leaks are a frequent issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. We examine the drain pan and surrounding area for moisture. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more destructive problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Johns Creek

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Johns Creek

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit.     Electric Water Heater Element Service in Johns Creek, GA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
