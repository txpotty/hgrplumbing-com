    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Johns Creek, GA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Johns Creek

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Johns Creek

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.
