    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Johns Creek, GA

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Johns Creek

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Johns Creek.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. We dig in from outside. A pit next to the foundation lets the crew tunnel under the slab by hand to the pipe, with shoring as needed, so your interior floors and cabinets stay untouched. Many Johns Creek, GA homes are good candidates.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Johns Creek

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Johns Creek truck.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Johns Creek

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Johns Creek homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.
