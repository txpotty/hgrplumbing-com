    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most practical solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the compromised section. Our technicians are skilled in careful concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is properly restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Georgia plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Johns Creek home.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Johns Creek, GA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Johns Creek, GA.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Johns Creek, GA

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Johns Creek

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Johns Creek with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
