    Pedestal Sink Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Dunwoody

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. Then the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Dunwoody

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Common sizes can go in the same day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Dunwoody

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
