    Shower Drain Body Repair in Dunwoody

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Dunwoody

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Dunwoody

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck.     Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Dunwoody

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Dunwoody, GA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port.
