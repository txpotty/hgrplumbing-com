    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Dunwoody, GA

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Shower Drain Repair in Dunwoody

    Slow shower drains are usually clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components when needed.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Dunwoody, GA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Dunwoody, GA

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Dunwoody, GA

    Some shower problems, like a burst pipe or a complete loss of water, need immediate attention. HGR Plumbing offers emergency shower repair services for urgent situations. We understand that plumbing emergencies never wait for business hours, and our team is ready to respond quickly to minimize damage and restore your shower, even outside of standard hours.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Dunwoody, GA

    Many homes in Dunwoody feature Moen or Delta shower fixtures, known for their quality but still subject to wear and tear. Our plumbers are very experienced in repairing and sourcing parts for these popular brands. Whether it is a leaky single-handle cartridge or a diverter issue, we have the specialized knowledge to fix your Moen or Delta shower correctly.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Dunwoody

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Dunwoody

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, comfortable temperature.
