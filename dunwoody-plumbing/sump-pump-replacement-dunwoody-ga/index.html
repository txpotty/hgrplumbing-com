    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Dunwoody

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Dunwoody

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Dunwoody for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Dunwoody

    A standard sump pump replacement usually costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are important, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.

    Key signs include the pump not turning on, unusual noises such as grinding or rattling, running continuously, visible rust or corrosion, or an age over 7 to 10 years. Any of these could point to coming failure and a need for replacement.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Dunwoody homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a smart investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm typically runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.
