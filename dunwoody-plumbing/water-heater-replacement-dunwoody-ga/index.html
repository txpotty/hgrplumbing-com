    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dunwoody, GA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Dunwoody, GA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Dunwoody, GA

    A standard tank replacement costs the least up front in Dunwoody, GA.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Dunwoody

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.
