    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Dunwoody

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. Plan on a full day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. Their Defender Safety System uses a sealed combustion chamber and a flame arrestor, so we follow their manual on clearances and the combustion air screen at the bottom of the tank. Thick steel, good parts. Code-approved installs every time.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Testing comes last. Value and reliability are why people choose them.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models. They never run out. Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so harder water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. HGR Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Then we test draft.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Dunwoody

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.
