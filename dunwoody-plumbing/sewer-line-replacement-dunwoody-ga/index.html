    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Dunwoody

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Dunwoody, GA

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rocky ground slows the dig. The estimate accounts for depth and soil once we have located the line. Each joint gets checked before the inspector sees it.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Dunwoody

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Dunwoody

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.
