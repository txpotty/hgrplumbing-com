    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Dunwoody

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Dunwoody, GA

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Older in-town homes often have galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.
