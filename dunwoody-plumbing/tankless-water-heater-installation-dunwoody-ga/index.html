    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Dunwoody, GA

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so more mineral-heavy water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
