    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Dunwoody

    The cost of well pump replacement in Dunwoody, GA typically ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary. Factors influencing the price include the type of pump, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or wiring upgrades. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough assessment.

    New well pumps typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. HGR Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Most well pump replacements are completed in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also cuts labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies widely depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will require replacement.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is highly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.
