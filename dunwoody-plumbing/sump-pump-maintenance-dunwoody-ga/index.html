    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Dunwoody

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Dunwoody due to their quiet operation and compact design.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Dunwoody

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Dunwoody residence.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Dunwoody

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Dunwoody, GA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff.     Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Dunwoody

    Over time, sump pits can collect dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch.     Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Dunwoody, GA

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Dunwoody, GA.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Dunwoody
