    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Dunwoody, GA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Dunwoody

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. Skip that step and the liner can wrinkle or fail to bond. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Pipe Bursting in Dunwoody, GA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Dunwoody

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Dunwoody

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Sectional Point Repair in Dunwoody

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Dunwoody

    Expect roughly $80 to $250 per foot for trenchless sewer repair in Dunwoody, GA, depending on the method and the pipe diameter. A 50-foot house lateral often lands between $4,000 and $12,500. That price covers labor and materials. Permits are listed separately.

    Workmanship and materials are typically covered for 10 to 20 years, depending on the product and the method. Liner makers add their own coverage. You get both in writing, along with the before and after camera video that shows what was installed and where.

    Pipe bursting uses high-density polyethylene, HDPE, fused into one continuous length with no joints. For lining, it is a felt or fiberglass tube saturated with epoxy resin. Both resist roots and corrosion, and both are rated by their makers for a service life of around fifty years when installed on properly prepared pipe.

    Much of Georgia sits on heavy red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. Soil that swells and shrinks with moisture puts stress on rigid pipe joints, which is how clay and cast iron lines crack and separate. Fused HDPE pipe bends a little with the ground instead of snapping. A cured liner also removes the joints that tend to fail first.

    It handles cracks, fractures, root intrusion and small offsets well. Pipe that has crumbled away completely is a different case. Severe offsets and deep bellies may need a dig, since a liner follows the shape of the host pipe and will copy the sag right along with it. The video decides.

    Decades. Quality liners and HDPE pipe carry a 50-year design life from most manufacturers when installed correctly.

    A rented drum machine might clear the clog for a week or two, but it will not fix a crack, an offset or the roots that keep growing back through a broken joint. Running a cable through fragile pipe can tear it further. A camera inspection is the smarter first step.

    Repeated backups, gurgling toilets, sewage odor, soggy patches in the yard and slow drains across the whole house are the common ones. Only a camera run tells you for sure what is going on.
