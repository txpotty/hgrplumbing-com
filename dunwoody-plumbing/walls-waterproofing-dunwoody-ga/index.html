    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Dunwoody, GA

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Dunwoody

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Dunwoody

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Dunwoody

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Dunwoody, GA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Dunwoody

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Dunwoody, GA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Dunwoody

    Crack injection runs about $300 to $800 per crack. A full exterior dig and membrane on a basement usually lands between $8,000 and $20,000, depending on depth, wall length and what sits against the house, while interior drain tile with a sump typically costs $4,000 to $12,000. Prices vary with access. We itemize every quote after the inspection.

    Anywhere from an afternoon to over a week. Interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while exterior excavation with new footing drains might run several days or longer than a week depending on the size of the house. Wet weather can push exterior work back.

    Patching one small crack yourself is fine. A full system involves excavation, digging equipment, proper membrane thickness and knowing how water drains around a footing, plus local code on drainage and backfill. A bad install can trap water against the wall and make things worse.

    It depends on the wall and the site. On exterior foundation work we use rubberized asphalt sheet membranes, polyurethane-modified asphalt liquids, bentonite panels and crystalline coatings, picking the one that suits the soil and the wall material. Inside showers, it is Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban.

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Crystalline coatings work well on poured concrete, but block walls usually call for an elastomeric membrane or drainage board.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them. Each manufacturer's install steps are followed exactly.

    A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, counting the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Bigger foundation jobs take several days, sometimes more if it rains.

    Most systems we install should go 20 to 30 years or longer. Life span comes down to how it was installed, what method was used and what the soil around the house is like. Sump pumps wear out sooner. Most pumps last seven to ten years.
