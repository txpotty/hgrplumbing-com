    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Dunwoody, GA

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Apron-front sinks rest on a support frame built into the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Dunwoody, GA

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Dunwoody

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Dunwoody, GA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Dunwoody, GA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Dunwoody, GA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. We test it all the way you will use it.
