    Picking a Tankless for Your Dunwoody, GA Home

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Dunwoody

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Long, steamy summers and heavy humidity are the main stress on plumbing in Georgia, driving condensation, mold in crawlspaces, and hard-working water heaters. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Dunwoody, GA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Dunwoody, GA

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Dunwoody, GA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. We price both.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Dunwoody, GA

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. We test both.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Dunwoody, GA

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. The pump runs on a timer. Mornings and evenings, usually.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Dunwoody, GA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Before a cold night, disconnecting hoses, covering hose bibs, and letting a faucet drip are usually enough to prevent a burst line. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.
