    Freezing temperatures are a leading cause of burst pipes, especially when uninsulated pipes are exposed to sustained cold. Other causes include extreme water pressure, corrosion from aging pipes, shifting foundations and physical damage from construction or tree roots.

    Common signs include a sudden drop in water pressure, visible water stains on walls or ceilings, musty odors, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, unusually high water bills or damp spots in your yard.     Watch for unexplained water bill increases, damp spots on walls or ceilings, warped flooring, musty smells and the sound of running water when fixtures are off. Your water meter can confirm a leak if it moves with everything turned off.

    Pinhole leaks typically result from water chemistry, high water velocity, excessive pressure or electrical grounding issues that corrode the pipe from the inside.
