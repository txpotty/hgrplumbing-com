    Pilot Light Repair in Marietta

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Marietta, GA

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Marietta, GA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Marietta

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to make for a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Thermostat & Gas Valve Repair in Marietta, GA

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is overheating, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components control the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Marietta, GA can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for fast and efficient repairs.

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Marietta

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Marietta

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.
