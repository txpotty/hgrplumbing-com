    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Marietta

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Marietta, GA

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500.     Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Marietta

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Marietta

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.
