    Jet Pump System Repair in Marietta, GA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HGR Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Marietta operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Marietta, GA

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but correct setup.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Marietta, GA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Coastal and low-lying parts of Georgia face flood risk during hurricane season, and saturated ground can push water into crawlspaces and basements. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Marietta, GA

    Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with clear numbers.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Marietta

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation often point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to find the problem. In Marietta, we repair or replace tanks to improve your well system's performance and extend pump life.
