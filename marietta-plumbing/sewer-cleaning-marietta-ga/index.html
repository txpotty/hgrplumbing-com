    What Our Marietta, GA Sewer Techs Handle

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Marietta, GA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Marietta

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Marietta, GA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Marietta

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. That adds time. A two-way cleanout by the foundation gives us access toward the house and toward the street, so future service is quicker, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Marietta, GA

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Tree Root Removal in Marietta, GA Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Then they fill the pipe. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Marietta, GA

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Several units, one pipe. We set up jetting on a schedule that fits your pump-out cycle and do the work when the building is quiet, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Marietta, GA

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. A line that has backed up before can be cleaned on a set schedule, often every year or two, so it never stops again without warning.
