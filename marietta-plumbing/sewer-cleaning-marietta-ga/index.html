    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Marietta, GA

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Houses from the late seventies through the mid nineties may still have polybutylene supply pipe, which is known to fail and is worth replacing. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a smart option.
