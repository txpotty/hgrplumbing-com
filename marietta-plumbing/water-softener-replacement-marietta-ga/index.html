    Picking the Right Softener for Marietta, GA Water

    Fleck Valve Programming in Marietta, GA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Marietta

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Marietta

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Marietta, GA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Marietta, GA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Marietta

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Marietta

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. Installs happen before opening or after close.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Marietta, GA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Georgia homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Local Water Softener Replacement in Marietta, GA

    A local plumber can also check how the softener ties into your water heater and main line. Leaking loops, missing air gaps and bypass valves left half open all hurt performance, and we catch them. Quotes are straightforward.
