    Sewer Camera Inspection in Marietta

    Every repair starts with a look inside. The camera goes in at the nearest cleanout.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Marietta, GA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Marietta, GA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. The southern coastal plain has looser, sandy soil and a higher water table, so drainage and root intrusion are bigger concerns there than shifting. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Marietta

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and provide inspection video that property managers can hand to owners or insurers. We can work outside business hours.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Marietta

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Marietta, GA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Marietta, GA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.
