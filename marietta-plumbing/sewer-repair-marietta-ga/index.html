    Sewer Pipe Relining in Marietta, GA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Marietta.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Marietta

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age.
