    Preventing Water Heater Damage in Marietta

    Thermal expansion is a leading cause of premature water heater failure in Marietta homes. Without an expansion tank, constant pressure swings stress the water heater's tank, connections and internal components, leading to leaks, reduced efficiency and a shorter lifespan. Our installation service solves this issue, providing a buffer that protects your investment. This small addition can save you thousands in future water heater repair or replacement.

    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Marietta

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and failing early.

    Tankless Water Heater Expansion Tank Integration in Marietta, GA

    While tankless water heaters typically have different pressure dynamics, an expansion tank can still be beneficial or even required in certain setups or by local codes in Marietta, GA. We assess your tankless unit and plumbing configuration to determine whether an expansion tank is necessary, especially with higher flow rates or a recirculation pump. Our technicians are experienced with integrating expansion tanks cleanly with many tankless models, keeping them running efficiently and protecting your plumbing system.

    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Marietta

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you steady pressure throughout the house.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Marietta, GA

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Standard Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Marietta

    Our standard installation service covers traditional tank-style water heaters, with the expansion tank correctly tied into your cold water supply line. We use high-quality, pre-charged diaphragm tanks built to absorb thermal expansion efficiently. This keeps excessive pressure from building up in your system, which is key for prolonging the life of your water heater and preventing damage to fixtures and appliances. We always test the system carefully after installation to confirm proper performance and compliance with local Marietta regulations.

    Residential Expansion Tank Installation in Marietta

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Marietta

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.
