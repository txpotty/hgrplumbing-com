    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Marietta

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Marietta, GA

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain in a short time, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Marietta

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house.     Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Marietta

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid. Pairing a submersible with a gasketed cover cuts noise and odor, and we route the discharge behind framing or through a closet so the pipe does not run across a wall you just painted in your Marietta home. We patch the slab flush.
