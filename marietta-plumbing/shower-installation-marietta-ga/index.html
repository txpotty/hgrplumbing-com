    Curbless Shower Installation in Marietta

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best.     Shower Stall Installation in Marietta

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base.
