    Gas Water Heater Installation in Marietta, GA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Marietta

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Marietta

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Marietta, GA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Marietta

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Marietta, GA

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless.     Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Marietta

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Marietta, GA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.
