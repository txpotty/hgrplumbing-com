    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Marietta

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Marietta

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Marietta, GA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HGR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Marietta

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Marietta

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Marietta, GA

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Marietta

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Marietta
