    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Marietta

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Marietta the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered.     Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Marietta

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. Fence posts and sprinkler trenches hit those lines more often than corrosion does. Dead grass is a tell. We pressure test the outdoor branch on its own, locate the leak and replace the run with polyethylene and a tracer wire. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Marietta

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Buried steel loses its coating to rocks in the backfill and then rusts through. Roots and settling do damage too. We call 811 for locates, dig at the suspected spot and replace the bad run with yellow polyethylene pipe and tracer wire. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Marietta trench rules.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Marietta, GA

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is relit.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Marietta

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. We schedule the work around your service hours when the leak allows it. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. We test every hose and manifold joint and leave tags on anything we had to shut off. New pipe still needs the commercial inspection.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Marietta

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. Regulator vents and lockup pressure get checked along with every joint. Rooftop units leak too. If a shutdown would cost you a dinner rush, we valve off just the bad branch and leave the rest live. Staff get a short walkthrough on the shutoff locations.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Marietta

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. In its place goes a coated stainless connector and a new shutoff valve, plus a drip leg if the code calls for it. Bubble testing follows before the pilot is lit.

    Gas Line Repair and Replacement in Marietta, GA

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Badly rusted sections, often near a crawlspace vent, are cut back to clean pipe and replaced. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a gauge test.
