    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Marietta

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Marietta

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Marietta

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Marietta is a smart measure against costly repairs. Our thorough check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, giving you recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Marietta, GA

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their robust build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to prevent damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a frequent area for attention in Marietta.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Marietta

    Proper ventilation is essential for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Marietta includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We make sure that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Marietta, GA

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in easier service.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Marietta

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. Our Rinnai-specific maintenance includes cleaning the air intake filter, checking the flame sensor and verifying the operation of the bypass valve. We also inspect the combustion chamber and confirm proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues early, improving performance for your Marietta home.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Marietta

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Marietta homes.
