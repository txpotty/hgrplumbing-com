    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Roswell

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge.     Urinal Brands We Install in Roswell, GA

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support.     ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Roswell

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Roswell, GA

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Roswell, GA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Roswell, GA

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Roswell, GA

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. The carrier, 2 inch waste, 3/4 inch supply and vent all go in to the spec sheet before drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.
