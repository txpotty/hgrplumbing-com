    Copper to PEX Conversions in Roswell

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. A switch to PEX ends it. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Roswell

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Roswell, GA

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Roswell, GA are good candidates for PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Rated walls need firestopping. Where PEX passes through a fire-rated assembly, we use listed firestop sleeves or sealant so the rating holds.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Roswell, GA

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Roswell, GA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
