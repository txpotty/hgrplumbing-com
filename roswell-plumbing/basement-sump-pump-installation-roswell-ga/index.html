    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Roswell

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Roswell, GA

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are strong and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Roswell, GA will install your submersible pump with precision, making sure of proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Roswell

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects.     While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves complex plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing.     Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain.
