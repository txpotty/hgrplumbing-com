    Before a cold night, unhooking garden hoses, covering hose bibs, and letting a faucet drip are usually enough to prevent a burst line.     Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Roswell, GA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. Look at it then or not at all. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Restaurant and Gym Bowl Replacement in Roswell, GA

    Gyms and restaurants break more bowls than offices do, usually at the rim or the bolt holes, and a broken bowl there means a restroom out of service. Work gets scheduled around your hours. We also check the carrier on wall-hung bowls, since a loose carrier cracks the next bowl too.
