    Resin Testing and Replacement in Roswell

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Roswell

    Scale on faucets means trouble. HGR Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, inspects every component and gives you a repair plan for your Roswell home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Roswell

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Roswell, GA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Roswell

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Roswell, GA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Roswell, GA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.
