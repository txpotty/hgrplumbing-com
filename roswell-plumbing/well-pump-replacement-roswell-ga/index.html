    Well Pump Sizing in Roswell

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Roswell, GA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. The suction line gets a pressure test. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Roswell, GA

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Roswell

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Roswell, GA

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    No-Water Calls in Roswell

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Roswell

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.
