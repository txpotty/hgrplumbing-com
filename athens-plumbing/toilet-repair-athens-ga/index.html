    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Athens, GA

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Leave that toilet alone until we look. The toilet comes up, the flange gets checked, and the bowl goes back on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Athens, GA

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot. On slab floors the flange is set in concrete and can crack if the closet bolts are overtightened. We check it. A cracked flange gets a stainless repair ring or a new flange before the toilet goes back down.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Athens, GA

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. If you still have a vinyl or solid plastic line, we change it to braided stainless before it lets go.

    Running Toilet Repair in Athens

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We look at the chain, float, flapper and seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Athens

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Sealants and epoxy are a stopgap at best on a tank that holds water every day. We replace it. A matching replacement tank is often an option, and it can cost less than a whole new toilet.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Athens, GA

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. Some can take an adapter flapper, and we carry a few kinds. For the rest, we will let you know whether parts can still be found or if replacement is the honest answer.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Athens, GA

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way. A gurgle from a nearby sink or tub when you flush points to a vent problem. We clear the trap with an auger and treat the jets with a descaler. A few first-generation 1.6 gallon toilets never flushed well, and we will tell you plainly if yours is one of them.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Athens, GA

You can usually see which one with the lid off. Our plumber watches a complete flush with the tank lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.
