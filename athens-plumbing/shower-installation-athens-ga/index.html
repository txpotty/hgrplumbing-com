    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Athens, GA

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Athens, GA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Usually a one-day job.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Athens

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Athens

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Athens, GA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Either one protects against scalding.     Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Athens
