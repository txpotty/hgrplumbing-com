    Sewer Relining and Pipe Bursting in Athens, GA

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Athens

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Athens

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Sectional Point Repair in Athens

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Athens, GA

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. An open-trench replacement can look cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Athens, GA

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Calls in Athens

    A sudden collapse usually shows up as every drain in the house backing up at once, often into the lowest tub or floor drain. The first job is getting flow back, usually with a cable or a jetter. Then the camera shows whether the line can be lined or needs a burst. A fully crushed section sometimes needs a short dig before anything else can happen.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Athens

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Athens

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.
