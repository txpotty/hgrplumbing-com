    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Athens

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in.     High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Athens, GA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Athens, GA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Athens

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.
