    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Athens

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Athens

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. Cracked china leaks eventually and heavy scale rarely comes clean, so a new bowl on the old carrier is usually best. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Athens

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Washbrook and Bardon models come in several flush ratings, and the valve has to match the bowl. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Athens

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Athens

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. Busy bars can see hundreds of flushes a night, so we install valves that recover fast and carriers that stay tight. We can fit tamper-resistant valves. Night installs keep you open.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Athens

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Athens

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.
