    Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Athens

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. Whistling points somewhere else. A relief valve or a half-closed supply valve is the usual cause.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Athens

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.
