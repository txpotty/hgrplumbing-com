    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Athens, GA

    Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so more mineral-heavy water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Drain Line Repiping in Athens

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. HGR Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Athens, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines.
