    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Athens, GA

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Athens

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Athens

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Athens, GA

    Sewage in the house cannot wait. Call (770) 470-3778. We open the cleanout to relieve pressure, camera the line to find the break, and make a section repair when that is enough to get the house working, then sit down with you about whether the rest of the line needs replacing.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Athens, GA

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and a root cutter clears them for a season at most, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Athens

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Athens, GA

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Athens, GA

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Athens

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. PVC avoids all three of those problems. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Local plumbers know local sewer layouts. Older lots sometimes drain to an alley main instead of the street. That affects depth, length and which method fits, and in Athens it is the kind of thing we usually know before we arrive. We still camera everything.
