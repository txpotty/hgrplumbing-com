    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Athens

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Athens

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most effective solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often required for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Athens, GA

    A collapsed sewer line can cause sewage backups into your home. Call (770) 470-3778 for emergency service. We inspect, locate the break and plan repairs quickly. Temporary measures may restore flow until replacement. Fast response limits damage.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Athens, GA

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Athens

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with targeted repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in Athens will conduct a complete camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution. We provide clear explanations and transparent pricing for both options.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Athens, GA

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are well suited for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Athens.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Athens, GA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Athens

    Many older homes in Athens still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are prone to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. HGR Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that lowers the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.
