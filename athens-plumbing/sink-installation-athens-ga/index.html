    Frequently Asked Questions About Sink Installation in Athens

    Installing a drop-in or pedestal sink usually runs $250 to $550 for labor. Undermount and vessel sinks typically cost $350 to $800, and adding a second sink or relocating a drain can push it to $900 to $1,800.

    We guarantee our installation labor, and the sink and drain carry the manufacturer's warranty. Details are listed on your paperwork.

    Most standard sink installations can be completed within 2 to 4 hours, assuming no major plumbing modifications are required. More complex installations, such as those requiring new water lines or drain rerouting, might take longer. We will give you an estimated timeframe during our first assessment.

    Common signs you might need a new sink include persistent leaks around the basin or drain, cracks or chips in the material, severe staining that won't clean, rust, or if you're simply upgrading for style or function. An old, damaged sink can also lead to water damage in your vanity.

    Most bathroom sink installs take two to four hours. Undermount sinks may need adhesive cure time before the drain is put to heavy use.

    HGR Plumbing can install a broad variety of sinks, including kitchen sinks (undermount, drop-in, farmhouse, bar), bathroom sinks (pedestal, vessel, console, integrated), and utility sinks. We handle installations for all standard residential and commercial settings.

    Many vessel sinks have no overflow, so the drain must be a non-overflow type, or air will not flow freely. We match the drain to the bowl to prevent this.
