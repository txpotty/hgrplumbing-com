    Complete Hot Water Tank Maintenance Services in Athens, GA

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Athens

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Athens, GA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Athens, GA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Athens

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Athens

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, preventing unexpected cold showers for your family in Athens.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Troubleshooting in Athens

    Even with regular maintenance, sometimes unexpected issues arise. HGR Plumbing offers emergency troubleshooting for hot water tank problems in Athens.     Sediment Flush and Descaling in Athens

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.
