    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Athens

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it.     Emergency Sewer Repair in Athens

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. In the meantime, we flag which drains to avoid.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Athens, GA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Athens

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, because wipes, grease and roots all call for different follow-up. Older Georgia homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Athens

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Athens

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Athens, GA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Athens

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.
