    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again after the next cycle.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Athens

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. HGR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Athens.

    Control Valve Repair in Athens

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. HGR Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Athens.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Athens, GA

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Athens, GA.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Athens

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Athens, GA

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Repair in Athens

    The cost of water softener repair in Athens usually ranges from $150 to $600, depending on the complexity of the issue and parts needed. Minor repairs like a clogged drain line or a simple control board reset might be on the lower end, while major component replacements such as a control valve or resin bed can be higher. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Many factors can cause a water softener to stop working, including a clogged drain line, a faulty control valve, a salt bridge in the brine tank, depleted resin beads, power outages affecting the timer, or mineral buildup in various components. Our diagnostics will find the exact cause.

    It's usually recommended to have your water softener professionally inspected and serviced every 1 to 2 years. Regular maintenance helps identify potential issues early, extends the lifespan of your unit, and ensures it operating at peak efficiency, preventing costly repairs down the line.

    Our technicians are trained and experienced in repairing all major water softener brands. This includes popular manufacturers like Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, Morton, EcoPure, GE, and Clack, among others. We have access to a wide range of replacement parts for various models.

    Typical signs include hard water symptoms returning (scale buildup, spots on dishes, dry skin and hair), the system using too much or too little salt, unusual noises during regeneration, leaks around the unit, or the system failing to regenerate at all. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional.

    Most repairs take one to two hours including testing. Resin replacement may take two to three hours depending on the size of the tank.

    While some minor issues like refilling salt or checking basic settings can be DIY, most water softener repairs involve complex plumbing, electrical components, or specialized parts. Attempting a DIY repair without proper knowledge can cause further damage or void warranties. We recommend calling a professional for reliable and safe repairs.

    If your water softener is leaking, right away turn off the water supply to the unit and unplug it (if electric) to prevent further damage. Then, contact HGR Plumbing right away. Leaks can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, or faulty seals, and quick repair is crucial to prevent water damage to your home.
