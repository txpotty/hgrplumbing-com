    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Athens, GA

    Slab leaks are particularly challenging when the soil under a home shifts and stresses the pipes below it. The southern coastal plain has sandier soil and a higher water table, so drainage and root intrusion are bigger concerns there than shifting. Our plumbers are experts in diagnosing and repairing slab leaks beneath your home's concrete foundation. We use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection to find the exact location, then offer solutions such as direct access repair, rerouting the line or trenchless techniques to fix the pipe with minimal impact on your property.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Athens

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can weigh both options.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Athens

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that section.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Athens, GA

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your system.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Athens

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is exposed.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Athens, GA

    Whether your home has modern PEX plumbing or traditional copper pipes, HGR Plumbing has the expertise to perform effective repairs. PEX is flexible and resistant to freezing, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Our plumbers are skilled in repairing both materials, using specific techniques and fittings designed for each type. We address issues like crimp failures in PEX or pinhole leaks in copper, using materials suited to Georgia climate.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Athens

Our team is skilled in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures.     Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Athens, GA

    A single pipe repair is much less expensive than a whole-house repipe and makes sense when the rest of the system is in good shape. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can quickly exceed repiping.
