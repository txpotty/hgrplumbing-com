    Water Softener Replacement in Athens, GA

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide consistently soft water throughout your Athens home.

    Homes in Athens, GA with older galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes often experience low water pressure, discolored water or frequent leaks. We carefully plan and complete repiping projects, minimizing disruption and making sure all new pipes are properly routed, insulated and connected, providing a durable and efficient plumbing system for decades to come.
