    Sewer Line Replacement Methods in Athens, GA

    Sewer Permits and Code in Athens, GA

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. Code covers pipe size, fall, bedding and cleanout placement. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Athens, GA

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. A resin-soaked tube is pulled into the cleaned host pipe and cured in place, which leaves a new pipe inside the old one and works well when the original still holds its round shape.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Athens

    Restaurants and strip centers cannot close for a week. We plan commercial sewer replacements around business hours, stage the equipment the night before and often work after close so the line is back in service by opening. Expect six inch pipe, sometimes larger. Heavy grease loads and high flow affect the pipe choice and the cleanout spacing.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Athens

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Athens, GA

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Trenchless methods ride the old pipe, sag and all, which means a real belly has to be dug out and relaid on grade. Rock adds time. We price that in after the locate shows depth. Each joint gets checked before the inspector sees it.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Athens, GA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Athens

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. A camera that dead-ends at broken clay or squashed fiber pipe means we have to dig, clean out the debris and replace that run. Being in the trench shows us what the surrounding soil is doing. Sometimes we find a washout or a root mat that explains why the pipe failed in the first place.

    How We Price Sewer Replacement in Athens
