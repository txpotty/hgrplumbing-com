    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Athens

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor.     Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Athens, GA

    Businesses in Athens, GA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house.     Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Athens

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Athens

    Residential ejector systems in Athens serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.
