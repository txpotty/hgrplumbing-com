    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Athens

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Athens

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Georgia homes.     Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Athens

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    HGR Plumbing can install all common types of kitchen sinks, including stainless steel, cast iron, composite, fireclay, and copper. We handle various mounting styles like top-mount, under-mount, and farmhouse (apron-front) sinks, delivering a professional fit for any material.

    While it's possible to attempt a DIY kitchen sink installation, it's usually not recommended unless you have significant plumbing experience.     Most installs take three to five hours. Undermount sinks may need time for adhesive to cure before the drain is fully loaded.

    A simple drop-in swap is doable for experienced DIYers. Undermount, farmhouse and double-bowl installs with disposals are more complex and prone to leaks.

    Signs you might need a new kitchen sink include persistent leaks around the drain or edges, visible cracks or chips, severe staining that won't clean, rust spots, or a desire to upgrade your kitchen's look. If your current sink is old or damaged, replacement is often the best solution.
