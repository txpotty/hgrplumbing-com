    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Athens, GA

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive.     Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Athens

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Athens, GA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.
