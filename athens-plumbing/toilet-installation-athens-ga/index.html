    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Athens

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, lower water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and simpler to clean.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom. We can help you navigate these choices.

    HGR Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style preferences, and water efficiency goals.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    Most standard toilet installations can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as wall-mounted or smart toilets, can take longer due to additional plumbing or electrical work.

    TOTO, Kohler and American Standard reliably perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    Generally, replacing an existing toilet with a similar model in the same location in Athens does not require a permit. However, if you are relocating a toilet, adding a new toilet, or making major changes to the plumbing system, a permit may be required. We can explain local code requirements.
