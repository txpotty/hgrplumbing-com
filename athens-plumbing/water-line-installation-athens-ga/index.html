    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Athens, GA

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. For long runs, HDPE is the tougher of the two, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Athens, GA. We keep PEX out of the sun.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Athens

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside until the pipe is half its original diameter. We replace them with PEX or Type K copper, trenched from the meter to the house, bedded in sand and tied into a new full-port ball valve at the foundation wall in Athens. Pressure comes back right away.

    Trenchless Water Line Boring in Athens

    Driveways and mature trees are good reasons to bore instead of trench. Solid rock limits boring.

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Athens
