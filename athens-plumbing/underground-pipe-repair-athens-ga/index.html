    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Athens

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. Full main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on how deep the pipe is.

    For underground repairs, we usually use durable materials like PVC, HDPE for trenchless methods or occasionally copper for certain water lines. These materials resist corrosion, root intrusion and ground shifts, providing a long-lasting solution that meets Georgia plumbing standards.

    For water lines we usually use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    HGR Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-approved materials from reputable brands known for durability and longevity. This includes PEX piping from manufacturers like Uponor or Viega, and various PVC and ABS pipe products that meet ASTM standards.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call for a professional inspection promptly.

    HGR Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the right repair techniques for each material.
