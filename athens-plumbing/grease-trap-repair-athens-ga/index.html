    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Athens, GA

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are essential for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Athens, GA, making sure your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Athens

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Athens, GA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving smoothly.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Athens

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a very effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, greatly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Athens business.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Athens

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are especially susceptible to grease build-up. In Athens, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions. Making sure these lines are clear is vital for the overall efficiency of your grease trap system.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Athens

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines.
