    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Athens

    The cost of drain repairs in Athens, GA can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all influence the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Tightening a loose slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a fair DIY job. If the leak is from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or inside a wall, a plumber can make a lasting repair and check for hidden damage.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also contribute to early failure.

    To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets. Regular professional drain cleaning every one to two years can also help prevent clogs and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your drains, recurring clogs or water backing up into fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is smart to call a professional before the problem worsens.

    Repeat clogs often come from grease buildup, hard water scale, poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a damaged pipe that catches debris. A camera inspection shows which cause you are dealing with so it can be fixed for good.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day. We give you a time estimate when we price the job.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also seldom clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.
