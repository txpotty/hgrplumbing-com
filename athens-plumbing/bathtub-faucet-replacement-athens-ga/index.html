    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Athens, GA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. A dated chrome cross-handle set can become a brushed nickel or matte black lever trim, and if the valve is a newer universal rough-in, the swap happens without opening the wall at all. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Athens, GA

    Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. At that stage the trim threads are often too weak and the valve body is pitted where the cartridge seals. Replacement is the better call.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Athens

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Athens

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Athens

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Athens, GA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Athens

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Athens, GA

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. We send the next open plumber. Broken stems are a common cause.
