    What We Fix on Well Systems in Athens, GA

    Jet Pump Replacement in Athens, GA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Athens

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Athens, GA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Athens

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Emergency Well Pump Service in Athens, GA

    Lost water at the house? Call (770) 470-3778 and tell us what the pressure gauge reads. Zero with the pump running points one way, zero with the pump silent points another, and that tells us which parts to load. We try to restore water the same day.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Athens

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. We check it on every pump job.

    Emergency Well Pump Replacement in Athens

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. The truck carries common 1/2 and 3/4 HP pumps. Splice kits and pressure switches ride along too.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Athens, GA

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. The storage tank does the job a battery would. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.
