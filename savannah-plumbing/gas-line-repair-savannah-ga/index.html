    Propane Gas Line Service in Savannah, GA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in Savannah

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. Once everyone is out and the gas company has secured things, we send a plumber to find the leak, fix it and test the system. We coordinate with the utility on the meter.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Savannah

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Savannah, GA

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain fast, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Savannah

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Savannah
