    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Savannah

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check.     Electric Water Heater Repair in Savannah

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect.     Pilot Repair in Savannah

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.
