    How We Replace Polybutylene in Savannah, GA

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Savannah, GA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Savannah, GA

    Some homeowners in Savannah, GA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Savannah

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Savannah

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Savannah, GA

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper runs higher. A yard service line typically adds $1,500 to $4,000, and that is still usually less than repairing damage from one burst.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX costs less. A planned replacement usually costs less than the drywall, flooring and cabinet repairs after a single burst, and that is before any deductible or mold cleanup gets added on.     Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. New PEX gets sized for the home and strapped to the frame. Exposed lines get checked. Northern and higher-elevation areas see more frozen-pipe calls than the south, where a deep freeze may only come every few winters. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.
