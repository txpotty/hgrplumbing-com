    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Savannah, GA

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Savannah

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Savannah, GA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Savannah

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Bigger is not always needed.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Savannah

    A large share of Georgia homes were built during the postwar and suburban growth decades, with copper supply lines and cast iron or early plastic drains. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Savannah

    Businesses in Savannah put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Savannah

    Electric water heaters in Savannah usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We help you compare.
