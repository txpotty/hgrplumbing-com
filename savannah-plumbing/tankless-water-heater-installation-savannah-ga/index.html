    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Savannah, GA

    Making the switch from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system involves more than simply swapping units. It often requires changes to gas lines, electrical wiring and venting. HGR Plumbing manages all aspects of this conversion, making sure all upgrades meet current codes and your new tankless unit performs at its best, giving you a modern hot water solution in Savannah, GA.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Savannah

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and haul away your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Savannah, GA

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan make up for part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas. We give you a side-by-side quote so you can decide what suits your budget.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Savannah, GA

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
