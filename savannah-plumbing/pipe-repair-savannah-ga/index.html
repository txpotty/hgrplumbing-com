    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Savannah, GA

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your plumbing.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Savannah, GA

    Before any repair can happen, the leak needs to be found. HGR Plumbing offers modern pipe leak detection services in Savannah, GA using non-invasive techniques. We use specialized equipment such as electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and inert gas tracers to precisely locate hidden leaks behind walls, under concrete slabs or underground. Our precise detection methods save you time and money by avoiding unnecessary demolition.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Savannah, GA

    Whether your home has modern PEX plumbing or traditional copper pipes, HGR Plumbing has the expertise to perform effective repairs. PEX is flexible and resistant to freezing, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Our plumbers are experienced in repairing both materials, using specific techniques and fittings designed for each type. We address issues like crimp failures in PEX or pinhole leaks in copper, using materials suited to Georgia climate.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Savannah

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can compare.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Savannah

    Older homes in Savannah often have galvanized steel or aging copper pipes that are prone to corrosion, leading to discolored water, low water pressure and frequent leaks. HGR Plumbing provides full corroded pipe replacement services. We can replace problem sections or re-pipe your entire home with modern, durable materials like PEX or new copper, significantly improving water quality and system longevity. We keep disruption to a minimum during the replacement process.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Savannah

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, commonly used in newer construction and remodels in Savannah. Our team is skilled in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures. We also offer PEX pipe installation, a reliable, corrosion-resistant and cost-effective solution for re-piping projects that delivers a leak-free plumbing system for years to come.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Savannah

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is open.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Savannah

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that section.
