    Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Savannah

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes usually come from grease, hair, soap scum and mineral scale.     Main Line and Sewer Drainage in Savannah, GA

    When several fixtures back up at once, the problem is most often in the main line to the city sewer or septic.     Dry Well Installation in Savannah, GA

    Where there is no place to discharge water on the surface, a dry well lets it soak into deeper soil. Dense clay or hardpan layers can block absorption, so we test the soil and dig through hardpan when needed. Some jurisdictions have rules on dry well registration and placement, and we follow those rules. A properly sized dry well with a sediment chamber can handle back-to-back heavy storms.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Savannah, GA

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff directly into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a safe outlet.

    French Drain Installation in Savannah

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after storms. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or approved discharge point.
