    From Tank to Drain Field: Septic Repair in Savannah, GA

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Savannah

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. Roots and settling ground cause most sags and breaks in that run. A camera from the house cleanout finds the bad joint, and we mark it in the yard. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Savannah

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Drain Field Repair in Savannah, GA

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Savannah

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and a dye test in the drains shows where water comes in or goes out. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Septic Tee Repair in Savannah

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Code and Permit Work in Savannah, GA

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout. Our office prepares the site sketch and submits the permit, then schedule the inspection before the trench is backfilled. Small fixes like a tee usually skip the permit. The inspector's sign-off goes in your file.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Savannah

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. Bad lids get replaced, and risers bring the opening up to grade. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.
