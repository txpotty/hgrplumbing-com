    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Savannah

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Savannah

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Pipe Bursting in Savannah, GA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Savannah, GA

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Savannah

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Savannah yard.
