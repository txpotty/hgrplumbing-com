    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Savannah, GA

    Older toilets are one of the biggest indoor water users in a home. WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so you save water without double flushing. Ask your water provider whether it now offers a toilet rebate.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Replacement in Savannah

    For modern bathrooms or those seeking to maximize space, wall-mounted toilets offer a sleek design. Replacing these units requires specific knowledge of in-wall carrier systems. Our team in Savannah has the expertise to carefully remove and install wall-mounted toilets, ensuring proper support, drainage, and water supply connections, keeping the look and function of your bathroom.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Savannah, GA

    Upgrade to a High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) with HGR Plumbing and start saving water in your Savannah, GA home. These models use significantly less water per flush, typically 1.28 gallons or less, compared to older 3.5 or 5-gallon models. We can help you select an HET that qualifies for potential rebates and provides strong flushing power, reducing your water bill and environmental impact without giving up performance.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Savannah

    Replacing a standard-height toilet with a comfort-height model raises the seat by about 2 to 3 inches. That small change makes a big difference for seniors and anyone with knee or back issues. We often pair this upgrade with an elongated bowl and soft-close seat. The swap uses the same drain location, so no extra plumbing work is required.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Landlords and property managers rely on us for durable, low-maintenance toilets in rentals. We can replace several toilets in one visit and standardize on a model with common parts.     Toilet Flange Repair and Replacement in Savannah

    Before installing a new toilet, it is essential to make sure the toilet flange is in good condition. A damaged or corroded flange can lead to leaks and instability. Our service for toilet replacement in Savannah includes inspecting and, if necessary, repairing or replacing the flange, providing a solid foundation and a leak-free seal for your new toilet fixture.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Savannah, GA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, which means fewer leak points and easier cleaning. They cost more and weigh more, but many homeowners like the clean look. We install one-piece models from TOTO, Kohler and American Standard. Our team handles the extra weight carefully to protect your floors.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Savannah

    Cracks in the tank or bowl can leak slowly or fail all at once. A hairline crack below the waterline is not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes seldom last. We replace the cracked toilet promptly and check the floor for any water damage. If only the tank is cracked and the model is still made, we can sometimes replace the tank alone.
