    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Savannah

    A soft wet patch near the sidewalk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most. We map the pipe with a line tracer and listen for the leak with a ground microphone, which keeps the dig to a single hole rather than a trench. Then we patch or replace.

    Gas Leak Detection in Savannah

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Leave the house if the odor is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Savannah, GA

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. We start outside. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Savannah, GA

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Savannah

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.

    Emergency Leak Detection in Savannah, GA

    Water coming through a ceiling at night does not wait for an appointment. Shut the main. We respond to Savannah, GA emergencies with full detection gear, find which line let go, and isolate it so you can get water back on the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Savannah

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. Utility coordination is included.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Savannah, GA

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.
