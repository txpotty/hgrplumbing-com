    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Savannah, GA

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Savannah

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Houses from the late seventies through the mid nineties may still have polybutylene supply pipe, which is known to fail and is worth replacing. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.
