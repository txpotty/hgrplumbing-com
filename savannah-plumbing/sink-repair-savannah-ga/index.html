    Common Sink Repairs in Savannah, GA

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Savannah, GA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Savannah, GA

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Savannah, GA

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Savannah, GA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Savannah

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Savannah

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Northern and higher-elevation areas see more frozen-pipe calls than the south, where a true hard freeze may only come every few winters. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Savannah

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.
