    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Savannah, GA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. Look at it then or not at all. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Replacing Stained Toilet Bowls in Savannah, GA

    Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. Iron and manganese in some water supplies leave orange or black stains that soak into worn glaze and will not scrub out once the surface is etched. Replacement is the only real fix. We recommend bowls with a fully glazed trapway, which stain far less and clean in seconds.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Savannah

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Cracked Bolt Hole Repair in Savannah

    Hairline cracks near the bolt holes often start when someone tightens the closet bolts too far, and they spread a little with every flush and every sit. Epoxy will not hold. We replace the bowl and use bolts snugged by hand, then finish with a quarter turn so the porcelain is not stressed again.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Savannah

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Hairline cracks still count. The bowl comes up so we can look at the flange and subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Changing Toilet Bowl Styles in Savannah

    Elongated bowls are about two inches longer than round ones and more comfortable for most adults, but they eat into the clear space code requires in front of the toilet. A compact elongated bowl can fit a tighter room. We measure the space before recommending one, so the door still closes.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Savannah

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. We look closely before quoting. A gasket and bolt kit fixes the first case, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume. The tank and bowl are engineered as a pair. Upgrading an old toilet usually means replacing both, and we will price it that way.
