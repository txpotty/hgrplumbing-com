    Backflow Testing and Certification in Savannah

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device.     Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Savannah

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months.     Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Savannah

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers.     Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Savannah, GA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Grease Trap Installation in Savannah
