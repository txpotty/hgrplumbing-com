    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Savannah

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Savannah

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Savannah

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor to the pump end and the wire size to the run length.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Savannah

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.
