    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Savannah

    The cost of sewage ejector pump installation in Savannah, GA typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000, depending on pump type, basin size, excavation needs and whether new electrical circuits are required. This estimate includes the pump unit, labor and materials. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after an on-site assessment.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. HGR Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent unexpected breakdowns.

    A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is built specifically to handle wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in areas below the main sewer line. They are not interchangeable and serve separate purposes.

    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspect your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.
