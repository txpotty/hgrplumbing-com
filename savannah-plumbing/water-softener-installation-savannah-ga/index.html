    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Savannah

    Accurate testing is key for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage.     Water Softener Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore full performance.

    Softener Loop Installation in Savannah

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Savannah

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. HGR Plumbing expertly installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Savannah home.
