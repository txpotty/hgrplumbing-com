    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Savannah

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Savannah

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. When the camera stops at a pile of broken clay or a flattened section of Orangeburg, the only fix is to dig down, clear the debris and lay new pipe through that stretch. Being in the trench shows us what the surrounding soil is doing. Sometimes we find a washout or a root mat that explains why the pipe failed in the first place.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Savannah, GA

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rocky ground slows the dig. We price that in after the locate shows depth. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Savannah

    No replacement quote without video. We run a self-leveling color camera from the cleanout to the main and record every joint, crack, root mass and low spot along the way, with footage you keep. A transmitter in the head lets us paint the spot and depth above ground. The footage tells us whether one section or the whole line needs to go.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Savannah, GA

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. If the camera shows a single offset or a cracked section and the rest of the line is round and clean, we dig one small hole at the marked depth and replace that piece with PVC and shielded couplings. That costs much less. You see the footage that supports the call.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Savannah, GA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Savannah, GA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time. If it has collapsed, we start permit and locate requests that day. HGR Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. That keeps one toilet and the kitchen sink usable while the dig gets scheduled.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Savannah

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Rough scale catches everything. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. For cast iron under a slab, we talk through lining versus rerouting.
