    Annual residential backflow testing usually costs $75 to $150 per assembly. Repairs with a rebuild kit usually run $150 to $450. New residential installation ranges from $300 to $1,200, and commercial RPZ assemblies often cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed.

    Backflow prevention is crucial because of changing water pressures, main breaks and fire flow that can drop pressure suddenly, and the potential for cross-connections from irrigation systems or industrial processes. These factors increase the risk of contaminated water entering the public supply, making a working backflow preventer a must.

    We service all major brands of backflow preventers, including Watts, Wilkins, Febco, Conbraco and Apollo, and Zurn. Our technicians are trained and equipped to handle various models and types, so your device receives expert attention and the proper parts for repair.

    A standard test usually takes 20 to 45 minutes per assembly. Water is briefly shut off during testing. Repairs may add an hour or more.

    Most water providers require testing at least once per year. Some require testing after installation, repair or relocation as well. Your provider's notice will list the due date.
