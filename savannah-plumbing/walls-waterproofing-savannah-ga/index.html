    Foundation Crack Repair in Savannah

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using proven epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks promptly in Savannah homes is crucial to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Savannah

    Many homes in Savannah have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Savannah

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Drainage System Integration in Savannah, GA

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Savannah, GA properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Savannah, GA

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. A large share of Georgia homes were built during the postwar and suburban growth decades, with copper supply lines and cast iron or early plastic drains. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Savannah, GA

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Savannah

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes keep most supply and drain repairs easier to reach. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Savannah, GA

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Savannah

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Savannah, GA can vary significantly based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump usually fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide transparent, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Walls waterproofing is a key step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help keep it that way.

    Shower remodels that change plumbing or drain locations often require a permit. Simple membrane replacement may not. We check local requirements and obtain permits when needed.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Savannah may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. HGR Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and handle the application process if needed.

    A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for many decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for long-term durability.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, full walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to poor results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days. A full exterior foundation waterproofing project, involving excavation around the entire home, could take 3 to 7 days or more. We provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.
