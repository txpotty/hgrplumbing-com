    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Brunswick

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Brunswick home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Brunswick, GA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Brunswick homes.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Brunswick, GA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Slab Leak Detection in Brunswick

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Brunswick, GA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Brunswick, GA homes are good candidates.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Brunswick, GA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Brunswick

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Brunswick homes with original plumbing see this often.
