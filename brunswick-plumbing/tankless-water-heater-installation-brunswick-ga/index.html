    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Brunswick

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Brunswick, GA

    Businesses in Brunswick, GA with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Brunswick, GA

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. Since the gas line and vent are already there, most swaps mean matching connections, inspecting the old vent and mounting the new heater. Different brands vent differently. We check before ordering.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Brunswick

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Brunswick

    Propane homes in Brunswick are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. A small tank can run short.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Brunswick

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Brunswick, GA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.
