    Softener Loop Installation in Brunswick

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Brunswick

    We also install Ecodyne water softeners, which are designed for solid performance and ease of use. These systems often come with user-friendly features and provide consistent soft water results. Our plumbers are familiar with Ecodyne specifications, delivering a correct and efficient installation for your Brunswick home.

    Water Softener Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore full performance.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Brunswick, GA

    Salt-based water softeners are the most common and effective type, using ion exchange to remove hard minerals like calcium and magnesium. HGR Plumbing specializes in the installation of these traditional systems, making sure of proper brine tank setup and regeneration cycles. We can recommend efficient models that reduce salt and water consumption for your Brunswick, GA home.
