    Softener Loop Installation in Brunswick

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Brunswick

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Water Softener Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Brunswick, GA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HGR Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Brunswick, GA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Brunswick, GA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Brunswick, GA

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops.     Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Brunswick

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.
