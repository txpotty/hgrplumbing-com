    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Brunswick

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Brunswick homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Brunswick, GA

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Brunswick, GA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Georgia homeowners.
