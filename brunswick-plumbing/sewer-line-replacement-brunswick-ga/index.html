    Sewer Camera Inspection in Brunswick

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the depth on the lawn. The footage tells us whether one section or the whole line needs to go.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Brunswick

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. When the camera stops at a pile of broken clay or a flattened section of Orangeburg, the only fix is to dig down, clear the debris and lay new pipe through that stretch.     Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Brunswick

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.
