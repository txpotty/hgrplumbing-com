    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Brunswick

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a clear drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Brunswick, reducing the need for extensive excavation.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Brunswick

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Brunswick

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Brunswick, GA

    Many older homes in Brunswick, GA still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for individual leaks in galvanized pipes where practical. Our plumbers are comfortable working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for eventual replacement.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Brunswick

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and longevity, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Brunswick, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our experienced plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We make sure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Brunswick, GA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.
