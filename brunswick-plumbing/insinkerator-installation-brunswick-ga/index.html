    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Brunswick, GA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Brunswick

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. The sink needs a disposal flange. The tailpiece and trap have to be reworked, and the cabinet needs a switched outlet, which means an electrician if there is no power under the sink already. Drain slope to the stack gets checked as well.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Brunswick, GA

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. We take them with us. That includes the old cord, the bent mounting ring if we replaced it and the corroded slip nuts, so nothing is left in a bag under your sink for you to deal with on trash day. One visit handles most of these.

    Evolution Series Installation in Brunswick

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Brunswick

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Brunswick, GA

    Cost differences come mostly from the unit and the prep work. A Badger 5 swap with a cord kit is at the bottom of the range. At the top is an Evolution Excel going into a kitchen that needs a new flange and a lower trap arm, with most jobs in between. You see horsepower and warranty length side by side.
