    Sump Pump Maintenance in Brunswick, GA: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Float Switch Inspection in Brunswick, GA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Brunswick, GA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Brunswick

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Brunswick

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Brunswick, GA

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Brunswick, GA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Brunswick, GA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. We clean their screens, test the floats and follow the discharge until it reaches daylight or an approved drain, because a line that dead-ends under the house just moves the water around. Hard freezes are infrequent in Georgia, but when a cold snap arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes in crawlspaces, attics, and exterior walls.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Brunswick

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Much of Georgia sits on dense red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time.
