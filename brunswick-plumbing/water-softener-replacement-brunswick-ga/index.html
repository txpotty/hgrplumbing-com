    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Brunswick, GA

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is typically the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem. We explain the cost of repair versus replacement clearly. Call (770) 470-3778 to schedule a check.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    For homeowners in Brunswick, GA looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Brunswick

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Brunswick

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and adjustable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, HGR Plumbing offers expert Clack water softener replacement in Brunswick. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, ensuring compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.
