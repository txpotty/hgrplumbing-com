    Picking the Right Softener for Brunswick, GA Homes

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Brunswick

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Brunswick

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Brunswick

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Brunswick

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Brunswick, GA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Brunswick

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. Installs happen before opening or after close.
