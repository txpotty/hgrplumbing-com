    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Brunswick

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. HGR Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. On a tankless unit, the culprit is often scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet filter, so we flush and clean before swapping parts.

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Brunswick, GA

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two. We camera the line and mark the depth and location of the problem on the surface. Repair options run from a traditional dig to pipe bursting or a cured-in-place liner. Every repair gets a final camera pass before the hole is closed.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Brunswick, GA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Neither one needs a trench across the driveway. Counting concrete and sod replacement, the cost often lands near an excavation. A camera decides.

    Slab Leak Repair for Brunswick, GA Homes

    The southern coastal plain has sandier soil and a higher water table, so drainage and root intrusion are bigger concerns there than shifting. Lines under a slab take the brunt of that movement, and Georgia homeowners often find out from a warm floor or a spinning meter. HGR Plumbing finds the leak with electronic equipment before any concrete is cut. Then the choice is a spot repair through the floor or a reroute that abandons the old line. Reroutes skip the jackhammer.

    Well Pump Service in Brunswick, GA

    Submersible pumps sit well below the water line, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. HGR Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. A pump drawing high amps is wearing out. Jet pumps are simpler, and most of the time a bad foot valve or a lost prime is to blame.
