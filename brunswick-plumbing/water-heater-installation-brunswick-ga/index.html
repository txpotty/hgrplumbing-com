    Gas Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Brunswick, GA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Brunswick

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. We know those manuals well. You can compare warranty length and recovery rate with us before you buy.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Brunswick

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank typically runs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless jobs run about $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and vent needs.

    Household size and peak demand decide the size. Two to four people usually do fine with a 40 or 50 gallon tank, while bigger families often need 60 to 80 gallons or a tankless unit sized by flow rate. Tankless goes by flow rate.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.

    A tank heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years with maintenance. Tankless units often go 15 to 20 years because they do not sit full of hot water and their parts can be replaced.

    It looks simple but involves gas, electrical and plumbing connections, plus a vent that has to carry exhaust out of the house every time the burner fires. A mistake can mean leaks, CO exposure or a fire. A licensed plumber is the safe call.
