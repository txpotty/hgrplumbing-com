    Curbless Shower Installation in Brunswick

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. The framing must be dry. Then the new shower goes in.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Brunswick

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Brunswick, GA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Brunswick

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit.
