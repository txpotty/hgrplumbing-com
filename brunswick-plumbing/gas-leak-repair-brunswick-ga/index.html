    Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a pressure test.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Brunswick, GA

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. We swap in a new coated stainless connector, add a quarter-turn ball valve and set a sediment trap where the code calls for it. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is relit.

    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Brunswick

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Brunswick the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered. We pull the permit and book the inspection ourselves. Most inspections take under twenty minutes. Test pressure and duration follow the local fuel gas code, which often means several psi for fifteen minutes or more. The signed permit card stays with your house.

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Brunswick, GA

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. It is also the stretch that takes the most weather, sitting in the sun or the soil. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for a typical riser swap.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Brunswick

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. Fence posts and sprinkler trenches hit those lines more often than corrosion does. Dead grass is a tell. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.

    Gas Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Brunswick, GA

    Price follows access. A connector or fitting repair at an exposed appliance usually runs $150 to $450, testing included. The written estimate comes after the leak is located and before any repair begins. Permit fees are listed as their own line.

    Gas Line Replacement and Upgrades in Brunswick, GA

    Adding a gas range or a tankless heater can overload pipe that was sized years ago for just a furnace and a water heater. Sizing starts with the total BTU load and the longest run from the meter. Undersized pipe starves burners. Old pipe with heavy rust or bad threads gets replaced instead of patched, usually with black iron or CSST depending on access. A big tankless unit may also need a larger meter from the utility.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Brunswick

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Buried steel loses its coating to rocks in the backfill and then rusts through. Roots and settling do damage too. We call 811 for locates, dig at the suspected spot and replace the bad run with yellow polyethylene pipe and tracer wire. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Brunswick trench rules.
