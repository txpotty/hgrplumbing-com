    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Brunswick, GA

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two. Most odor from these units comes from a bad gasket.
