    Essential Plumbing Maintenance Services in Brunswick, GA

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Brunswick

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Brunswick

    Older in-town homes often have galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside, while newer construction mostly uses PEX or CPVC. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Brunswick, GA

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Brunswick

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Brunswick, GA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Gas Line and Repipe Inspections in Brunswick, GA

    Safety is critical with gas lines, and our maintenance includes thorough inspections for leaks, corrosion, and proper valve operation. For older homes in Brunswick with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipes, our repipe specialists can assess the need for replacement. Regular checks help identify potential hazards or signs of coming pipe failure, preventing dangerous situations and significant property damage.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Brunswick, GA

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Brunswick, GA

    Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain fast, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Brunswick.
