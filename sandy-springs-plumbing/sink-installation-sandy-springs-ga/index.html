    How We Set New Sinks in Sandy Springs, GA

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Sandy Springs

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Sandy Springs, GA

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Sandy Springs

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Sandy Springs

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. For an accessible bathroom we set the rim no higher than 34 inches, leave knee clearance underneath, and wrap or shield the hot water and drain pipes so a seated user cannot touch them. Offset drains help with knee space. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.
