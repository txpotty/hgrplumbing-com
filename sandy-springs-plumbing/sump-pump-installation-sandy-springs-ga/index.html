    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Sandy Springs

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Sandy Springs, GA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Sandy Springs

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Sandy Springs

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Sandy Springs

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Sandy Springs

    Not every home needs one. Homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or yards that collect runoff get the most from a pump, since storm water has nowhere else to go.
