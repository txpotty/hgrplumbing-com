    Annual residential testing runs $75 to $150 per assembly. A rebuild usually costs $150 to $450. New residential installs range from $300 to $1,200, and commercial RPZ assemblies often cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed once the drain and enclosure are included.

    Water providers may send follow-up notices, charge fees or eventually suspend service. Booking early heads all of that off. We can often fit urgent tests in on short notice, even when the deadline is only a few days away.

    Look for a steady leak from the device, a sudden drop in water pressure or discolored water at the tap. An RPZ that discharges from its relief port whenever pressure shifts usually has a leaking check. Have it looked at.

    Water dripping or running from the relief valve on an RPZ is the clearest sign. Low pressure at the house, a cracked bonnet after a freeze or water that tastes or smells off can also point to the assembly. Have it checked promptly.

    A brass assembly often lasts 10 to 20 years, sometimes longer. The rubber parts inside wear out much sooner, which is why rebuild kits exist and why most failed tests are fixed without replacing the body. Freezes shorten that life.

    Watts, Wilkins, Febco, Conbraco, Apollo and Zurn are all on our list. Our technicians carry kits for the popular models in each line, and less common parts can usually be ordered within a few days. Older models are usually fine.
