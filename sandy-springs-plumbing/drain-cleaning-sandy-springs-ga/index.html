    Jetting Versus Cabling in Sandy Springs, GA

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Sandy Springs

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.
