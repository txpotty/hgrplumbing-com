    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables.     Direct Slab Leak Repair in Sandy Springs, GA

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Sandy Springs

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Sandy Springs homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Sandy Springs, GA

    Commercial slabs in Sandy Springs carry longer pipe runs and more branches. Locating takes longer. Branches get isolated one by one, and the cutting is booked after closing or on a weekend so customers never notice. Work meets Georgia code and stays within code.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Sandy Springs

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Sandy Springs, GA
