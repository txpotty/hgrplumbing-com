    Workmanship on pipe repairs is warranted for the term on your invoice. New pumps carry their own coverage. Seepage repairs carry terms that match the method, and if water returns at the same spot during the warranty period we come back, retest it, and fix it without charging for the labor.

    Usually not, unless the leak is at an exposed fitting you can reach. A loose compression nut or a worn washer hose is fine to fix yourself. Soldering overhead, cutting cast iron or chasing a hidden leak takes tools most homeowners do not have. Guessing gets expensive.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Wet cracks get polyurethane. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing is broader. It handles groundwater all around the foundation and makes sense when water comes in at many points.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Keep them clear and extended well away from the foundation. Test the sump each spring and swap rubber washer hoses for braided ones every five years.
