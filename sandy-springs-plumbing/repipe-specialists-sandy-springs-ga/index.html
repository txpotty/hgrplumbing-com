    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Sandy Springs, GA

    Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so more mineral-heavy water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply.     PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Sandy Springs

Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.
