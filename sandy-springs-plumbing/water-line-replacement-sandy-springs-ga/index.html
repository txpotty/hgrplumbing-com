    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Sandy Springs, GA

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Sandy Springs

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house.
