    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Sandy Springs

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Sandy Springs

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.
