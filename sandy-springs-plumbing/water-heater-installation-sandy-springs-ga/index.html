    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Sandy Springs

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing.     Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them very efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O.     Water Heater Installation Cost in Sandy Springs, GA

    Tank water heater installations generally cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting.     Expansion Tank Installation in Sandy Springs, GA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.
