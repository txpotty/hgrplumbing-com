    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Sandy Springs

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Sandy Springs

    Power outages during heavy storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides crucial protection when the power goes out. We install reliable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an important safeguard for homes in Sandy Springs that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We integrate these systems seamlessly with your existing plumbing.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Sandy Springs, GA

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Sandy Springs

    The cost for basement sump pump installation in Sandy Springs, GA typically ranges from $800 to $2,500, depending on the type of pump, pedestal or submersible, whether a new pit needs to be excavated and whether a battery backup is included. We provide detailed estimates after an on-site assessment.

    A standard basement sump pump installation usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

    Typically, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage. Some policies offer separate water backup or sump pump failure endorsements for an additional premium. It is best to check with your insurance provider.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, built to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for the best protection.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is usually not allowed by local codes.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump every 2 to 3 months, especially before and after the rainy season. Simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to make sure the pump activates, drains the water and shuts off properly. Regular testing helps catch issues early.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves real plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing. Incorrect installation can lead to pump failure, electrical hazards or poor water removal, which can cause serious flood damage. It is always best to hire a licensed plumber for a proper, code-compliant installation in Sandy Springs.
