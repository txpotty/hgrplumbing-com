    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Attic and crawlspace temperatures climb quickly in summer, which speeds up wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping run through those spaces. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.
