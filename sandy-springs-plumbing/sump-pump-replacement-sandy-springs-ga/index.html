    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    Zoeller pumps are renowned for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Sandy Springs, GA homeowners. HGR Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a high-quality, long-lasting unit. We can replace your existing Zoeller pump with a comparable model or upgrade you to a more powerful one to meet your needs and local conditions.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Sandy Springs, GA

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a smart habit.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. HGR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Sandy Springs, GA so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Sandy Springs, GA, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Sandy Springs

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Sandy Springs home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.
