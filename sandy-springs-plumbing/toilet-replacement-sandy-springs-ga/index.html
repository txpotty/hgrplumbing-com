    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Sandy Springs

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips.     Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Sandy Springs, GA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery.     Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Sandy Springs

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.
