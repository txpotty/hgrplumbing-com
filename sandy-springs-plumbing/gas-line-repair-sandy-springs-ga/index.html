    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Sandy Springs

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down.     Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Sandy Springs

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it.     Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Sandy Springs

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Sandy Springs, GA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas.     CSST Repair in Sandy Springs

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Sandy Springs

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up.
