    Sump Pump Maintenance in Sandy Springs, GA

    Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain in a short time, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Sandy Springs.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Sandy Springs

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Sandy Springs

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Sandy Springs

    A large share of Georgia homes were built during the postwar and suburban growth decades, with copper supply lines and cast iron or early plastic drains. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Sandy Springs, GA

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still wise. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Before a cold night, disconnecting hoses, covering hose bibs, and letting a faucet drip are usually enough to prevent a burst line. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance significantly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.
