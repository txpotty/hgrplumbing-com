    Leak Detection and Assessment in Sandy Springs, GA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes make most supply and drain repairs easier to reach.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Sandy Springs

    Private wells still serve a large share of rural Georgia homes, and iron, sediment, and low pH can shorten the life of pumps, tanks, and copper lines. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.
