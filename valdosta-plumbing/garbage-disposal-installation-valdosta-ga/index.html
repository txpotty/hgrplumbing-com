    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Valdosta

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Valdosta, GA

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other.
