    Where We Find Leaks in Valdosta, GA

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Valdosta

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes keep most supply and drain repairs easier to reach. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Slab Leak Detection in Valdosta

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Valdosta, GA

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. Your water bill is usually the first warning. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and check framing with a moisture meter, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, which keeps the drywall cut small in your Valdosta home.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Valdosta

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Pool Leak Detection in Valdosta

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Valdosta, GA

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Valdosta

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Valdosta, GA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.
