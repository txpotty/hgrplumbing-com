    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Valdosta

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Valdosta, GA

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models. They never run out. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Valdosta, GA

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. HGR Plumbing installs the full Rheem line. We connect the gas, set the flue and check the Guardian flammable vapor sensor and the combustion air path so the safety features work as designed. Then we test draft.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Valdosta

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.
