    Pressure-balance valves keep the hot and cold ratio steady when pressure changes. Thermostatic valves set the temperature directly and add volume control, a good fit for multi-head showers.

    Both stop scalding, but they work differently. Pressure-balancing valves keep the ratio steady, and thermostatic valves hold a set temperature regardless of pressure. The thermostatic kind is pricier.

    A pressure-balancing valve watches pressure. When someone flushes a toilet and the cold side drops, the valve cuts hot flow to match, so the mix stays close to where you set it. A thermostatic valve watches temperature itself. It suits showers with several outlets.
