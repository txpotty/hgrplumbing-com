    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Brookhaven

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Leach Field Restoration in Brookhaven, GA

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Brookhaven, GA

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid.     Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Brookhaven, GA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.
