    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Brookhaven

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Brookhaven, GA can vary a lot, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Look for unusually wet spots in your yard, unexplained increases in your water bill, foul odors, decreased water pressure or gurgling sounds from drains. These can all indicate a hidden leak or blockage in your underground plumbing system.

    HGR Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-approved materials from trusted brands known for durability and longevity. This includes PEX piping from manufacturers like Uponor or Viega, and various PVC and ABS pipe products that meet ASTM standards.

    Yes. Our repairs include a written workmanship warranty, and the pipe and fittings carry manufacturer warranties. We explain coverage before we begin.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    For underground repairs, we usually use durable materials like PVC, HDPE for trenchless methods or occasionally copper for certain water lines. These materials resist corrosion, root intrusion and ground shifts, providing a long-lasting solution that meets Georgia plumbing standards.
