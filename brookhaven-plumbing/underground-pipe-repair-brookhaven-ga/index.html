    Main Water Line Repair in Brookhaven

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Small leaks grow.     Pipe Bursting Services in Brookhaven, GA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Brookhaven, GA

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the cheapest option. Bursting runs higher. We price all three.
