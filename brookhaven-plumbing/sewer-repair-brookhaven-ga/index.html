    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Brookhaven

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example.     Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Brookhaven, GA

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Brookhaven

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Brookhaven

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Brookhaven, GA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.
