    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Brookhaven

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Georgia, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are adept at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Brookhaven

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Brookhaven, GA

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from strong winds, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. HGR Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and maintaining good indoor air quality.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Brookhaven, GA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Brookhaven.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Brookhaven, GA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Much of Georgia sits on dense red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Brookhaven

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.
