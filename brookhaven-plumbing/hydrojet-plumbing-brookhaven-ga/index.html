    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Brookhaven, GA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Brookhaven

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Brookhaven

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Brookhaven, GA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Long dry spells matter too, because clay soil pulls away from pipes in drought and roots chase moisture into cracked sewer laterals. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Brookhaven, GA

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Brookhaven

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Brookhaven

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.
