    Curbless Shower Installation in Brookhaven

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Shower Pan Installation in Brookhaven

    A correctly installed shower pan is essential for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor. HGR Plumbing expertly installs various shower pan types, including pre-fabricated units and custom-tiled pans with proper waterproofing membranes. We make sure the pan is level and correctly sloped toward the drain, a vital step that prevents expensive water damage in the long run. We use high-quality sealants for lasting protection.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Brookhaven, GA

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Brookhaven

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Brookhaven

    While installation is often planned, sometimes a damaged or severely leaking shower requires prompt replacement. HGR Plumbing provides emergency shower replacement services to promptly address critical failures. We can often install a new, standard shower unit to restore your bathroom without delay.     Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach of the seat. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan.     Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Brookhaven, GA

Proper valve installation prevents scalding and ensures consistent water temperature, a critical comfort feature for your home in Brookhaven.
