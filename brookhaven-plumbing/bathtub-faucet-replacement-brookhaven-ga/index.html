    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Brookhaven

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Brookhaven

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Brookhaven, GA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Brookhaven, GA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. A dated chrome cross-handle set can become a brushed nickel or matte black lever trim, and if the valve is a newer universal rough-in, the swap happens without opening the wall at all. Matching the sink faucet is easy. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Brookhaven

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Brookhaven

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. We track that on your invoice. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.
