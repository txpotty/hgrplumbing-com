    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Brookhaven, GA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Brookhaven, GA

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Brookhaven, GA

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.
