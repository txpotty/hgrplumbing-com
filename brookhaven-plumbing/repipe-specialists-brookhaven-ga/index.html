    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Brookhaven, GA

    PEX Repiping in Brookhaven, GA

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Brookhaven, GA

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Brookhaven, GA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. The numbers add up quickly. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Brookhaven

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Brookhaven, GA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Brookhaven, GA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Brookhaven

    Older commercial buildings in Brookhaven often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. We replace supply risers, branch lines and drains while keeping offices, shops or apartments running as much as possible. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. We plan the work by zone and handle keys, access and quiet hours with your manager.

    Drain Line Repiping in Brookhaven

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.
