    PEX Pipe Replacement in Brookhaven, GA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. HGR Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Brookhaven, GA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup.
