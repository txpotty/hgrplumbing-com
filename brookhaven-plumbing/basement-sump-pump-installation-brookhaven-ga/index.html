    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are strong and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Brookhaven, GA will install your submersible pump with precision, ensuring proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, dependable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Brookhaven

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Brookhaven

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water effectively. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Brookhaven property.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Solutions in Brookhaven, GA

    For homes with significant groundwater issues or large basements, a standard sump pump may not be enough. HGR Plumbing installs high-capacity sump pump systems designed to move larger volumes of water quickly and efficiently. These systems often feature more powerful motors and larger discharge lines. We conduct a thorough assessment of your property to recommend and install a system that can handle even the heaviest rainfall.
