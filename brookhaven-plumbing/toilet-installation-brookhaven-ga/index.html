    Types of Toilet Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    Standard Toilet Installation in Brookhaven

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Brookhaven

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Brookhaven, GA

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Brookhaven

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Brookhaven

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks. Often the broken unit comes out and a 12 inch rough-in replacement goes in during the same trip, round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. We bring the flange up with a stacking extender or replace it outright, and if you are moving the toilet on a slab, that means cutting concrete to reroute the drain and patching it afterward. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Brookhaven, GA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.
