    Sewer Line Materials and Brands in South Fulton, GA

    We work with a variety of high-quality sewer line materials and trusted brands for durability and performance. Options include PVC (polyvinyl chloride) for its corrosion resistance, ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) for its strength, and sometimes modern cast iron for specific applications. We source our pipe from established manufacturers that meet industry standards. Much of Georgia sits on heavy red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. Our team helps you choose the best material for your situation and budget.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in South Fulton

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, especially along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that snags waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in South Fulton

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Sewer Line Replacement Cost in South Fulton, GA

    Cost depends on length, depth, soil type, access and the method used. Open-trench replacement in an open yard can be reasonably priced per foot, while trenchless methods may cost more per foot but save on restoration. Hardscape, landscaping and driveways change the math quickly. We give you a written estimate for each option after a camera inspection. That way you can compare total cost, not just the headline price.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in South Fulton and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.
