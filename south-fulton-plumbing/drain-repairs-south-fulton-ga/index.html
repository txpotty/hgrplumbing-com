    Drain Vent Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in South Fulton

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, plus tub and shower drain leaks that stain the room underneath. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. The southern coastal plain has looser, sandy soil and a higher water table, so drainage and root intrusion are bigger concerns there than shifting. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Vent Pipe Repair in South Fulton

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Recurring Clog Repair in South Fulton

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. The fix goes where the clog starts.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. No drain clogs more often. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct trap heights so the arm has the right fall to the wall. The disposal and dishwasher hookups get a look too.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Drain seals wear out. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, and failed drain gaskets can usually be replaced from above without cutting into the ceiling below. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.
