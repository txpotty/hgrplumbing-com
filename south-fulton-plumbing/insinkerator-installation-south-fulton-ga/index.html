    Badger Disposal Install in South Fulton

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in South Fulton

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Badger Series Installation in South Fulton, GA

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Hard Water and Disposals in South Fulton

    Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so harder water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. Heavily scaled parts get replaced the same visit.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in South Fulton, GA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in South Fulton

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. We test both appliances at once.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in South Fulton, GA

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank. Clay soil can make drain fields slow to absorb water, which shows up as soggy yards, slow drains, or sewage backups after heavy rain. InSinkErator's Septic Assist feeds an enzyme solution into the chamber during each run to start breaking down waste before the tank. We mount the cartridge and show you how to swap it. Scraping plates into the trash still helps.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in South Fulton, GA

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. The SoundSeal insulation cuts motor noise. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. With the motor running, we check the mount for vibration.
