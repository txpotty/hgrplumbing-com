    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in South Fulton

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in South Fulton, GA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in South Fulton, GA

    Many South Fulton, GA homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. HGR Plumbing provides essential maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a common concern during severe storms.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in South Fulton

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in South Fulton, GA

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in South Fulton

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear.
