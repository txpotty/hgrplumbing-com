    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in South Fulton

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in South Fulton, GA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in South Fulton, GA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in South Fulton, GA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in South Fulton

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in South Fulton. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HGR Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.
