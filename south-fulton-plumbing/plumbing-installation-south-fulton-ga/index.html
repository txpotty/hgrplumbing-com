    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Installation in South Fulton

    Price depends on the job more than anything else. A standard water heater install runs about $1,200 to $2,500, while a new gas line can be $800 to $3,000 or more depending on length and how many appliances it feeds. Access also moves the number. We give a written quote after seeing the site.

    Private wells still serve a sizable share of rural Georgia homes, and iron, sediment, and low pH can shorten the life of pumps, tanks, and copper lines. Softened water keeps scale out of the pipes, the water heater and the dishwasher, and scale is what kills those appliances early. Soap also lathers more easily. Expect to cut detergent use noticeably.

    Reddish-brown water is the big one. Low pressure, recurring leaks and original pipe in a home past 50 years old all point the same way, toward a repipe. In an older South Fulton home, original galvanized steel is the usual cause. We can cut out one fitting to show you how much the inside has closed up.

    PEX is cross-linked polyethylene tubing for water supply lines. It does not corrode, it tolerates a freeze better than copper, and it goes in faster since it bends where copper needs a fitting. It is a dependable choice for Georgia homes, with fewer fittings and fewer leak points. We mostly install PEX-A with expansion fittings.

    Tank heaters cost less up front. Tankless units give continuous hot water and often last longer, but they need more gas or electrical capacity.

    A heater or softener install typically takes a few hours. Plan a day for a gas run or water line, and several days for a repipe.

    A sump pump moves clear groundwater out of a pit in the basement or crawl space. An ejector deals with waste, solids included, and lifts it from below-grade fixtures up to the sewer main or septic tank. You cannot swap one for the other.

    Venting, gas connections and the relief valve are where DIY installs fail. A licensed plumber builds it to code and keeps the manufacturer warranty intact.
