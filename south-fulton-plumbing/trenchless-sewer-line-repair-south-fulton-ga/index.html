    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in South Fulton

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in South Fulton that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in South Fulton

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Root Intrusion Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.
