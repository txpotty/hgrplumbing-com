    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in South Fulton

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    A sudden sump pump failure can lead to immediate flooding, especially during heavy rainfall. HGR Plumbing offers emergency sump pump replacement in South Fulton, GA to address urgent situations. Our rapid response team will diagnose the problem and replace your faulty pump with a new, reliable unit. We work to limit water damage and restore your peace of mind as quickly as possible.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in South Fulton, GA, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    For added protection, many homeowners in South Fulton choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. HGR Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in South Fulton

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in South Fulton for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in South Fulton

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times.     Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in South Fulton

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.
