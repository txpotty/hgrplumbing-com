    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in South Fulton, GA

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in South Fulton

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in South Fulton

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in South Fulton, GA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in South Fulton

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. The run from the sinks to the trap is where grease cools and builds up. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in South Fulton

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. Most of the time it is a torn lid gasket or a dry floor drain nearby.
