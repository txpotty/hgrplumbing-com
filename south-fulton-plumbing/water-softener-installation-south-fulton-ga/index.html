    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in South Fulton

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in South Fulton, GA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Carbon tanks cost extra. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Water Softener Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in South Fulton, GA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in South Fulton

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in South Fulton, GA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in South Fulton, GA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HGR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in South Fulton

    Most South Fulton, GA softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. That typically covers the unit itself and the labor to plumb it in, program it and test it, with any loop or drain work listed separately. Bigger homes cost more. We give written pricing up front.

    Manufacturer coverage on the unit is often 5 to 10 years on major parts. Our installation workmanship is usually covered for a year, and we install to the manufacturer specs so their warranty stays valid.

    On an existing loop, installs typically take two or three hours. A new loop adds a few hours.

    With a loop in place, some homeowners can do it, but air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Hiring a plumber avoids leaks and poor results.

    White, chalky scale on faucets and dishes is the most common sign. Dry skin after showering, dull laundry, weak lather and lower water pressure from pipe buildup all point the same way. You likely have hard water.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. Once a year a plumber should check for salt bridges, worn seals and injector clogs. Do not let the salt run out.

    A softener targets calcium and magnesium to stop scale. A filter removes things like chlorine and sediment to improve taste and safety, and plenty of homes use both together.

    Ten to fifteen years is typical for a quality softener. Unit quality, how hard your water is and how well the system is maintained all change that, and resin can be replaced to extend it. Yearly checks help.
