    Sump Pit Installation in South Fulton, GA

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Less cycling, longer life.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in South Fulton

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in South Fulton

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.
