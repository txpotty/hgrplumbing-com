    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in South Fulton

    Residential hydrojetting in South Fulton typically costs $400 to $900, including a camera inspection before and after. Snaking costs less per visit. Because jetting removes the buildup instead of punching through it, many homeowners end up spending less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking for the same line.

    It is safe for most sound PVC, ABS, cast iron and clay. Pressure varies by pipe type. Thin-wall pipe, old Orangeburg fiber pipe and any line with cracks or separated joints get lower pressure or no jetting at all, which is why the camera inspection happens before we ever turn the jetter on.

    It depends on the line. A cart unit handles most houses. Bigger lines get a trailer unit, because flow carries debris and pressure breaks it loose.

    Jetting fits when the same line clogs again and again. The camera shows the buildup. If the footage shows heavy grease, scale or roots coating the pipe wall, jetting is the right fix, but if it shows a broken or collapsed section, the line needs repair first.

    We use cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID. Each job gets its own nozzle. We carry penetrating nozzles for packed lines, spinning nozzles for grease and scale, and root-cutting heads, plus a camera with a locator so we can mark any defect on the surface.

    Plan on two to three hours for a typical home. That covers both camera passes. Restaurants and larger buildings can take half a day or more, and we try to schedule those before opening or after closing so the kitchen or the tenants are not affected.

    We do not recommend it. The equipment is dangerous. High pressure can cut skin, and jetting blind can break a weak pipe.

    We guarantee the line will flow freely when we leave. The service warranty is written on your invoice. If the camera shows a defect that could cause a clog to return, such as a belly or a root-filled crack, we point it out before we start so the terms are clear and nobody is surprised later.
