    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in South Fulton

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. A single bad joint can usually be dug and fixed. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet tell us the line is failing everywhere, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in South Fulton

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. Restaurants usually have a grease interceptor in the line too. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and work with the utility on the main connection. Weekend starts are common.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in South Fulton, GA

    Much of Georgia sits on dense red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, which can lead to uneven settling of the slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and compact the backfill in lifts. Slow work near the house.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in South Fulton

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in South Fulton

    Digging is still the right method for a lot of lines. A collapsed pipe cannot guide a bursting head. Sharp bends, a belly that needs regrading, or a route that should change all point toward an open trench, and we leave the yard as close to how we found it as dirt allows. Grass grows back.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in South Fulton

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. We have two ways in. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or dig a tunnel beneath the foundation from the yard, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in South Fulton

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. Modern PVC does not rust, flatten or let roots in at glued joints. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.
