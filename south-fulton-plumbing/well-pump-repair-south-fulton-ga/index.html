    Pressure Switch Replacement in South Fulton

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Jet Pump Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in South Fulton is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Jet Pump System Repair in South Fulton, GA

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HGR Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in South Fulton operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in South Fulton

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Pressure Tank Repair in South Fulton, GA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in South Fulton
