    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in South Fulton, GA

    Electric Element Service in South Fulton, GA

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. We test with power off. Good parts stay in.

    Anode Rod Checks in South Fulton

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in South Fulton

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks.     Descaling Tankless Units in South Fulton

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.
