    Picking a Tankless for Your Stonecrest, GA Home

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Stonecrest

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Stonecrest, GA

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Stonecrest, GA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Hard freezes are occasional in Georgia, but when a cold snap arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes in crawlspaces, attics, and exterior walls. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Stonecrest

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Stonecrest

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Stonecrest

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Stonecrest, GA

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.
