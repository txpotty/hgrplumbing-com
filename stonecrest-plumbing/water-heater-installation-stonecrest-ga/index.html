    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Stonecrest

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Stonecrest

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Each has its own install manual, and following it to the letter is what keeps the tank warranty valid if something fails in year six. We know those manuals well. We help you compare models on warranty length and recovery rate.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Stonecrest

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
