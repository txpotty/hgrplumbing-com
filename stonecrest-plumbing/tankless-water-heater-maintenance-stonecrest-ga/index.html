    Annual Tankless Checkup in Stonecrest, GA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Stonecrest

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Stonecrest

    Private wells still serve a sizable share of rural Georgia homes, and iron, sediment, and low pH can shorten the life of pumps, tanks, and copper lines. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Noritz Descaling in Stonecrest, GA

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Stonecrest

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Stonecrest, GA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Stonecrest

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.
