    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements.     Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Stonecrest

    Residential ejector systems in Stonecrest serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Businesses in Stonecrest, GA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Closed restrooms cost business. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Stonecrest

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. The float usually goes first.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. That speeds up new installs, and their wide-angle float switches are less likely to hang up on the basin wall than older tethered floats, which is one of the most common reasons a pump fails to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Stonecrest

    Zoeller's sewage line runs from light residential to heavy duty, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Bigger lifts need more.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. Builders like the timing.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Stonecrest

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier.
