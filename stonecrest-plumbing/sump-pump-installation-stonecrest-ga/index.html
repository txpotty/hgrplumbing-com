    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Stonecrest

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Stonecrest

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Stonecrest, GA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Stonecrest, GA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Stonecrest

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Stonecrest, GA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Sump Pump Installers Near You in Stonecrest, GA

    Local soil and the way Stonecrest, GA homes were built both change how a sump system should go in. HGR Plumbing has set pumps in clay, sand and rock. In clay the water stays against the foundation longer, so the pump runs more often and a larger basin helps the motor last. That changes the pump we pick.
