    Lining and Bursting in Stonecrest, GA

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Stonecrest

    Older Stonecrest homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Stonecrest, GA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Stonecrest, GA soil.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Stonecrest

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. A sectional liner is cheaper and quicker for a short stretch of damage, though we camera the rest of the line first to be sure it is sound.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Stonecrest

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Stonecrest yard.
