    What Goes Into a Sewer Lateral Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Stonecrest, GA

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rock adds time. The estimate accounts for depth and soil once we have located the line. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Stonecrest

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Stonecrest, GA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. A typical burst takes one working day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Stonecrest

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. Only two pits get dug. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Stonecrest

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. A camera that dead-ends at broken clay or squashed fiber pipe means we have to dig, clean out the debris and replace that run. Digging also lets us see the soil around the break. Often there is a void or a root mat right at the failure point.

    Sewer Codes and Inspections in Stonecrest, GA

    Inspectors check what gets buried. Plumbing work in Georgia is licensed at the state level, so it pays to verify that whoever works on your home holds a current license. They look at sizing, fall, bedding, cleanouts and how the new pipe ties into the city side. We book the inspection and meet the inspector. Work done without a permit can mean digging it back up to show the inspector, which nobody wants.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Stonecrest

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Rough scale catches everything. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. For cast iron under a slab, we talk through lining versus rerouting.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Stonecrest, GA

If the camera shows a single offset or a cracked section and the rest of the line is round and clean, we dig one small hole at the marked depth and replace that piece with PVC and shielded couplings.
