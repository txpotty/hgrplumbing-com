    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Stonecrest

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.
