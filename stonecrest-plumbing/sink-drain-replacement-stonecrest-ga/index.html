    Sink Drains We Replace in Stonecrest, GA

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Stonecrest

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Stonecrest

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Stonecrest

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Cost of Sink Drain Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    Scope sets the price. Bath pop-up and trap swaps cost the least, and kitchen drains or rusted wall stubs cost more. Designer finishes add some. The quote lists each part.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Stonecrest

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Stonecrest

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Lint and grit wear out thin utility tub drains and pack the trap so it never drains right. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.
