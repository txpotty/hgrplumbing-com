    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Stonecrest, GA

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Stonecrest

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Stonecrest

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.
