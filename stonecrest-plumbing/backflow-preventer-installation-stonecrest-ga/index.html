    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Stonecrest

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Stonecrest

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Stonecrest, GA

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Before a cold night, unhooking garden hoses, covering hose bibs, and letting a faucet drip are usually enough to prevent a burst line. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
