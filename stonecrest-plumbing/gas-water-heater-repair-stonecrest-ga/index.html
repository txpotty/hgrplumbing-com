    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Stonecrest, GA

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label.     Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Stonecrest

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water and years without a flush both make it worse. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. Whistling points somewhere else. A relief valve or a half-closed supply valve is the usual cause.

    Burner Assembly and Venting Repair in Stonecrest

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. The burner comes out, the ports get cleared and the flame arrestor gets a look. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Stonecrest home. We hold a smoke pencil at the draft hood to see which way the air moves.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Stonecrest

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    Thermostat & Gas Valve Repair in Stonecrest, GA

    Scalding water at the kitchen tap is a valve problem until proven otherwise. Sensors can drift as they age, and dials sometimes get bumped past the hot mark. A setting near 120 degrees works for most households.
