    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Atlanta, GA

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Atlanta

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Atlanta

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Atlanta, GA

    A drip at the top usually means the sink flange putty has dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Atlanta, GA

    Most jams and small leaks are much cheaper than a replacement. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. You make the call.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Atlanta

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Atlanta, GA

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. Motors on these units are not sold separately.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.
