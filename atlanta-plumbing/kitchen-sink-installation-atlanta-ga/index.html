    Workstation Sink Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are a common pick in modern kitchen remodels.

    Composite Sink Installation in Atlanta

    Composite sinks, made from materials like granite or quartz composites, offer excellent durability and a wide range of colors to match your kitchen decor. Installing these sinks requires careful handling to prevent damage and get a perfect fit. Our plumbers in Atlanta are experienced with composite sink installation, using appropriate tools and techniques for secure mounting and sealing. We ensure all drain and faucet connections are tight and leak-free, providing you with a long-lasting and attractive kitchen fixture.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Farmhouse or apron-front sinks add a striking look to any kitchen, but their installation requires specific expertise due to their weight and unique mounting requirements. HGR Plumbing provides careful farmhouse sink installation, ensuring the sink is adequately supported within your cabinetry and properly aligned with your countertop. We carefully manage the plumbing connections, which can differ from standard sinks, to prevent leaks and keep efficient drainage. This specialized service gives you a beautiful and functional focal point for your kitchen.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Atlanta

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Commercial kitchens in Atlanta have unique demands, requiring heavy-duty sinks that can withstand constant use and meet strict health codes.     Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Atlanta

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Atlanta

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Georgia homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still crucial to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Atlanta install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.
