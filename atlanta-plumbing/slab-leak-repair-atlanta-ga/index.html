    Finding and Fixing Slab Leaks in Atlanta, GA

    Slab Leak Reroute in Atlanta

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Atlanta

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Atlanta homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Slab Leak Detection in Atlanta

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Drain lines under the slab leak too. Those leaks smell. A camera inspection shows the crack, sag or roots, and the repair might be a tunnel, a slab cut or a liner, depending on location and length. Health codes in Atlanta, GA apply to every drain repair.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Atlanta, GA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Atlanta homes.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Atlanta

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Atlanta home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.
