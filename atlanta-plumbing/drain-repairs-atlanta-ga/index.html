    Bathroom Drain Repair in Atlanta

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Hard water makes it worse. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Atlanta

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, and where a vent was never installed we can add an air admittance valve in locations where local code permits it. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Atlanta

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. The footage tells us what to fix.

    Commercial Drain Repairs in Atlanta

    Commercial drains see more grease, more paper and more flow than any house. A closed restroom costs business. We repair grease-coated kitchen lines, floor sinks, mop sink drains and restroom branches for Atlanta businesses, working early mornings or after hours to keep you running. We also set up maintenance schedules.

    Drain Vent Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.

    Burst Drain Line Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Inside a wall, a broken drain line can soak flooring fast. Shut off the fixture. When you call with an active leak, we move the visit up, isolate the damaged section and make a permanent repair whenever the access and parts allow it, which is most of the time. We tell you about wet drywall right away.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, and failed drain gaskets can usually be replaced from above without cutting into the ceiling below. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.
