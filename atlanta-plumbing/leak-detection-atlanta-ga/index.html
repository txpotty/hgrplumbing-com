    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Atlanta, GA

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to serious drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to precisely pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's walls and ceilings.

    Slab Leak Detection in Atlanta

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use dedicated acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you significant repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines under the slab.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Atlanta

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to spot temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, neat access opening.

    Pool Leak Detection in Atlanta

    A leaking pool can lead to wasted water and higher utility bills. Our specialists are experienced in detecting leaks in pool liners, plumbing and equipment. We use dye testing, pressure testing and listening devices to find the precise source of the leak, whether it's in the skimmer, main drain or return lines. Protect your investment and enjoy your pool without endless refilling in Atlanta.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Atlanta, GA

    Leaks in underground water lines, often leading from the meter to your home, can result in significant water loss and property damage. We use ground microphones and tracer gas detection to locate these buried leaks precisely. Factors like tree roots, ground shifting and corrosion in older galvanized pipes can contribute to these issues, making professional detection important.
