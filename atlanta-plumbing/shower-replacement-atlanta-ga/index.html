    Shower Valve Replacement in Atlanta

    Old two- and three-handle shower valves often leak and lack anti-scald protection. We open the wall from the back or front, remove the old valve and install a modern pressure-balance or thermostatic valve. Where possible, we use a larger trim plate to cover the old opening. This upgrade improves safety and comfort.

    Tile Shower Replacement in Atlanta, GA

    Tile showers can fail when grout cracks or waterproofing breaks down. We handle the plumbing side of tile shower replacement, including valve, drain and pan.
