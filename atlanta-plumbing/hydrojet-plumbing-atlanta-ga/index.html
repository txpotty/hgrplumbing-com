    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Atlanta, GA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Atlanta, GA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Atlanta, GA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Atlanta, GA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Coastal and low-lying parts of Georgia face flood risk during hurricane season, and saturated ground can push water into crawlspaces and basements. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Atlanta

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Atlanta, GA

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Atlanta

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Atlanta, GA

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.
