    Locating and Fixing Foundation Leaks in Atlanta, GA

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Atlanta

    Not every wet foundation is a plumbing leak. Gutters cause plenty. Downspouts that dump right at the wall and soil that slopes toward the house push water against the foundation, and that pressure finds every crack in the concrete. We fix grading, extend downspouts and install French drains around Atlanta homes when needed.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Atlanta

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. HGR Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Atlanta foundation is sound again.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Atlanta, GA

    Basement walls in Atlanta, GA leak through cracks, tie holes and the joint where the wall meets the floor. Injection fixes cracks. Single cracks take a polyurethane or epoxy injection, and a wall that seeps at the base usually needs interior drain tile running to a sump.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Look for wet insulation. Water sitting under the house rots sill plates and floor joists and feeds mold on the subfloor, so we find the source, fix the pipe and check whether the vapor barrier needs replacing. A sump pump may make sense in a Atlanta, GA crawl space that floods from outside.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Older homes in Atlanta, GA often have soft copper running under the slab. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. Pinholes form where it touches gravel. The bad section comes out, new Type L copper goes in with sweated or pressed joints, and the full line gets a pressure test before any dirt goes back.

    Commercial Foundation Leak Repair in Atlanta

    Restaurants, offices and retail spaces in Atlanta get slab leaks too, and HGR Plumbing handles them. Every closed hour costs you. Work is staged so only the affected branch is off, and concrete cutting can happen overnight to keep your doors open. Perimeter waterproofing is an option on larger buildings.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Atlanta

    Locating comes before anything else. First we isolate hot from cold. Each side gets its own pressure test, and once we know which side is losing water, we follow the pipe route with a tracer and listen along it with the ground mic until the sound peaks. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.
