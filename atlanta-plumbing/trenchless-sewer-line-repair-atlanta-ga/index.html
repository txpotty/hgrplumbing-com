    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Atlanta

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Atlanta that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be used to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. This preserves your home's interior and reduces renovation costs.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Atlanta

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is essential. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior.     Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Atlanta

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Atlanta, GA

    Sewer lines in Atlanta, GA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Much of Georgia sits on heavy red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.
