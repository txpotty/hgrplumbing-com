    Main Service Line Leaks in Atlanta, GA

    The service line carries full street pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil is often the only clue. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair, usually suggesting replacement when the line is old galvanized or polybutylene.

    Commercial Water Leak Detection in Atlanta, GA

    Offices, shops and apartment buildings in Atlanta have long supply runs that hide leaks. Downtime costs money. HGR Plumbing isolates each zone and tests risers, mains and branch lines with pressure gauges, then locates leaks with acoustic and thermal tools, working around your schedule so business keeps going.

    Slab Leak Detection in Atlanta

    Concrete slab homes hide supply lines under the floor, where a pinhole can leak for months unseen. Warm floor spots are the classic sign. HGR Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides with pressure gauges, then listens through the slab with a ground microphone and confirms with a thermal camera, so the spot is marked before any concrete gets touched.

    Underground Water Line Leaks in Atlanta

    The buried line from the meter to the house can leak for a long time without surfacing. Look for green stripes in a dry lawn. HGR Plumbing traces the pipe with an electronic locator, listens for the leak with a ground microphone and switches to tracer gas on plastic lines, which keeps the dig to one hole rather than a trench.

    Smart Leak Detection Devices in Atlanta

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn watch your water flow and can close the main on their own when they see a leak pattern. Sensors go by water heaters and washers. We install and set up the system so alerts come to your phone, and some insurers give a premium discount for having one.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Atlanta

    The meter is the first and simplest leak test. Everything off, then watch. If the leak indicator spins with every fixture and appliance off, water is escaping somewhere, and the flow rate gives a rough count of gallons lost per day. Then we shut off zones one at a time.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Atlanta

    Sprinkler and drip lines run underground across the whole yard, and they hide a lot of water loss. Stuck valves are common. We isolate the irrigation system from the house, run each zone and trace leaking lines, since a cracked lateral or broken emitter can lose thousands of gallons unnoticed.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Atlanta

    Copper leaks ring clearly on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter. Plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas in homes plumbed with it, paying special attention to crimp fittings, the usual weak point. We also find many copper pinholes caused by water chemistry.
