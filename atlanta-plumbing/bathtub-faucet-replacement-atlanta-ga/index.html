    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Atlanta

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else.     Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Atlanta, GA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Atlanta, GA

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A new cartridge and a matching trim kit from the same brand can make a twenty-year-old single-handle faucet work and look nearly new for a fraction of what a valve replacement costs. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Atlanta, GA

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.
