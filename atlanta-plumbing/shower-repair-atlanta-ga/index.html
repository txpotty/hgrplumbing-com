    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Atlanta

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Atlanta

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers promptly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will make sure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Atlanta

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can promptly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge.     Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.

    Shower Drain Repair in Atlanta

    Slow shower drains are typically clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components when needed.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Atlanta

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.
