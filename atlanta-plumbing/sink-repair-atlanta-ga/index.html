    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Atlanta, GA

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Atlanta

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Atlanta

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Northern and higher-elevation areas see more frozen-pipe calls than the south, where a deep freeze may only come every few winters. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Atlanta

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Atlanta, GA

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Atlanta

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. A down hand sink can cost you an inspection, so we work around your hours and carry commercial faucets and basket strainers. Grease is the usual enemy. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Atlanta

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.
