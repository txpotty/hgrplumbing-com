    A common culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Atlanta

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent.
