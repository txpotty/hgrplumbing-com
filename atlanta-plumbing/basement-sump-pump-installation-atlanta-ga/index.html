    The Sump Pump Work We Do in Atlanta, GA

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Atlanta, GA

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. Those basements need a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower pump in a larger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Atlanta

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid. A submersible with a gasketed cover keeps the noise down, and we route the discharge behind framing or through a closet so the pipe does not run across a wall you just painted in your Atlanta home. The concrete patch ends up flush with the floor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Atlanta homes. Expect more noise.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Atlanta

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It is a 3/10 horsepower unit with a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Atlanta basements. Parts are easy to get.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Atlanta

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. We add a spring check valve so the water in the pipe cannot fall back, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Before a cold night, disconnecting hoses, covering hose bibs, and letting a faucet drip are usually enough to prevent a burst line. Where lines can freeze, we fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Atlanta

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. That is where a pedestal pump earns its place, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. They are noisy but durable.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Atlanta, GA

    Pricing starts with whether you already have a pit. A pump swap in an existing basin costs the least, while a new pit adds saw cutting, digging and a concrete patch, and a battery backup adds the second pump and battery on top of that. Each line is itemized.
