    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Atlanta, GA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Atlanta, GA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HGR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Atlanta

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Atlanta, GA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Filters add cost. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Atlanta

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. A loop saves time.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Atlanta

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Atlanta, GA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.
