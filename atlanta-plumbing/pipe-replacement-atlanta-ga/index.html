    PEX Pipe Replacement in Atlanta

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Northern and higher-elevation areas see more frozen-pipe calls than the south, where a true hard freeze may only come every few winters. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. HGR Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Atlanta

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Atlanta, GA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.
