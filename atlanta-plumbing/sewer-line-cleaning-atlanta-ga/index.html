    What Our Atlanta, GA Sewer Line Crews Handle

    Clearing Household Laterals in Atlanta

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots lead the list. Wipes, grease and roots cause most household clogs, and a camera tells us which method will work best, so the fix lasts.

    Large Commercial Laterals in Atlanta, GA

    Big commercial laterals carry more solids. Volume adds wear. We use trailer jetters with higher flow to clean 4, 6 and 8 inch lines, with extra attention on grease interceptor outlets and long runs to the city main, plus written reports for maintenance logs.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Atlanta

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Roots grow back. A regular cleaning schedule keeps roots and grease in check, especially on older lines near big trees.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Atlanta, GA

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. A jetter scours the full circumference of the pipe at pressures we set for the material, from PVC to clay.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Atlanta

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. Both build up slowly. Over time, grease and hair coat the pipe and catch more debris, especially in older cast iron with a rough inner wall, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in Atlanta

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. The southern coastal plain has looser, sandy soil and a higher water table, so drainage and root intrusion are bigger concerns there than shifting. They grow fast inside. Root saws on a sectional cable cut the mass loose, and a jetter follows to remove fine root hairs and bring the pipe back to its full diameter, and we explain options to keep them out.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Atlanta

    A camera shows the line in real time. Problems become obvious. We look for low spots where water pools, offset joints, cracks, roots and corrosion, and a locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the surface, with a copy of the video for you.

    Maintenance Plans for Sewer Lines in Atlanta, GA

    Maintenance plans cost less than backups. Schedule them ahead. Periodic jetting removes grease, sludge and early root growth before it becomes a problem, which extends the life of the sewer system and avoids surprise backups.
