    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Atlanta, GA. A spring-loaded air inlet opens when the pressure falls, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Atlanta

    Fertilizer and herbicides in drip and sprinkler lines are exactly what a backflow device keeps out of your drinking glass. Local rules decide whether it is a PVB or a double check, along with the layout of your system. PVBs go 12 inches above the highest head.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Atlanta

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. Two spring-loaded checks and four test cocks are inside, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. We size for flow.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Atlanta, GA

    High-hazard connections in Atlanta, GA call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. That means chemical feed systems and medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. We plan for the discharge.

    Backflow Preventer Brands We Install in Atlanta

    Watts, Wilkins, Apollo and Conbraco cover most of the assemblies we install. All four have repair kits on supply house shelves, which matters when a check fails its annual test and the tester wants to rebuild it that day instead of ordering parts and coming back in Atlanta. Common parts keep costs down.
