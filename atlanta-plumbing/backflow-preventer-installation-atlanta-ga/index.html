    Reliable Backflow Preventer Installation Services in Atlanta, GA

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Atlanta

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Emergency Backflow Preventer Replacement in Atlanta

    While installations are usually planned, sometimes an existing backflow preventer fails suddenly, creating an urgent need for replacement in Atlanta. A compromised backflow device can lead to immediate contamination risks or code violations. HGR Plumbing offers emergency replacement service to promptly assess the situation, remove the faulty unit and install a new, code-compliant backflow preventer, restoring your water safety as fast as possible.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Atlanta, GA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Atlanta, GA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Atlanta, GA

    For high-hazard applications in Atlanta, GA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HGR Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code.
