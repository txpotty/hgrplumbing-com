    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Atlanta

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Atlanta, GA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Atlanta

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Atlanta

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Atlanta, GA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. We list each option on a written quote.

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta

    A pump swap in an existing pit usually runs $600 to $1,500. New pits, with saw cutting, excavation and a concrete patch, run about $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups add $500 to $1,500.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Plenty of cities ban discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    Test it every month by pouring a bucket of water into the pit. Once a year, scoop out silt, check the float for free movement and walk outside to make sure the discharge outlet is clear of leaves and ice. Takes ten minutes.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. If we have to cut the slab, dig a new pit and patch the concrete, plan on most of a day, with the patch curing overnight. A simple swap is quicker.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. A pump that cycles every few minutes through a wet spring wears out faster than one that runs a few times a month, and cheap switches fail first. A quick inspection tells us where it stands.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. Anything involving a new pit, a new discharge route or new wiring is a job for a professional.

    A straight replacement often needs no permit. New pits, electrical work or new discharge connections may need one, and we will tell you.

    Permits depend on the scope of the job and the local building department. Georgia builds its plumbing rules on the International Plumbing Code with state amendments, and local building departments handle permits and inspections. HGR Plumbing pulls any permit the job needs in Atlanta.
