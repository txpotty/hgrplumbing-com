    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Atlanta, GA

    For complete peace of mind, consider a combination sump pump system. This setup includes a primary electric sump pump and an integrated battery backup pump in a single unit, providing comprehensive protection against both power failures and primary pump malfunctions. HGR Plumbing can install these dual-pump systems in your Atlanta, GA home, offering stronger flood prevention. This solution is ideal for homes in flood-prone areas or those with expensive basement finishes.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Atlanta, GA

    If your existing sump pump fails during a storm, you need quick help. HGR Plumbing offers emergency sump pump replacement services across Atlanta, GA.     New Sump Pit Installation in Atlanta

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is skilled in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Atlanta, GA.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Atlanta, GA

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that alert you to problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Atlanta

    A high water alarm lets you know when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are especially useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Many older homes in Atlanta, GA have crawl spaces that are vulnerable to moisture issues and standing water. Installing a dedicated sump pump in your crawl space can control groundwater and prevent humidity problems, mold growth and structural damage. HGR Plumbing has experience with difficult crawl space installations, including proper vapor barrier integration and discharge line routing to protect your entire home's foundation.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Power outages during heavy storms are a serious concern, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system takes over on its own if your primary pump loses power, ensuring continuous flood protection. We install reliable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Atlanta.
