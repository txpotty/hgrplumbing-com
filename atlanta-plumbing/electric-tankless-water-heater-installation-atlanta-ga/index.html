    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Atlanta

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run fairly soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. A softener or scale filter helps, and the service valves we install make yearly descaling simple. It is a short service.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Atlanta

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Warmer water means more flow. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.
