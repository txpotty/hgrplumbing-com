    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Atlanta, GA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Atlanta, GA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Atlanta, GA

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Atlanta home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Atlanta

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Atlanta

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs quick attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Atlanta

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which particular line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, giving you an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Atlanta, GA.
