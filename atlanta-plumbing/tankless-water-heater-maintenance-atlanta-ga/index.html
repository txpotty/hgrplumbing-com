    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Atlanta

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Atlanta homes.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Atlanta

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Atlanta

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Atlanta, GA

    A tankless descaling visit usually costs far less than replacing a scaled heat exchanger, which can run $600 to $1,500 or more in parts and labor. Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for. We help you decide whether a softener or scale inhibitor would reduce your long-term costs too.
