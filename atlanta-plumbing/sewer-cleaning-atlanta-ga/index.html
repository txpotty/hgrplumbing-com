    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Atlanta

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Atlanta, GA

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Atlanta, GA

    Commercial lines take a beating. Restaurants fight grease every day. Offices and retail buildings deal more with paper towels and foreign objects flushed in public restrooms, which snag on joints and catch everything after them, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Atlanta, GA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Atlanta

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. It makes every call slower. A two-way cleanout by the foundation gives us access toward the house and toward the street, so future service is quicker, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Atlanta, GA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Atlanta, GA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.
