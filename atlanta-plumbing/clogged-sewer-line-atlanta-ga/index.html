    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Atlanta

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes snag on the coating and roots growing through a joint hold the whole mass in place until the line stops. We clear it with a sewer machine, then check on camera to see the main cause.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Atlanta

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. We see what the pipe looks like. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. That keeps digging to a minimum. You get a copy of the footage.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Atlanta

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Atlanta, GA

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Atlanta, GA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. You see the footage first.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Atlanta, GA

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up. Soap scum and food bind to it until the opening shrinks to a fraction of the pipe, after which ordinary use causes backups. HGR Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Atlanta, GA. Then we show you on camera.
