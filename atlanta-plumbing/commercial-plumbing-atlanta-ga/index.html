    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Atlanta

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Atlanta, GA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Atlanta

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. The southern coastal plain has looser, sandy soil and a higher water table, so drainage and root intrusion are bigger concerns there than shifting. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Atlanta

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Atlanta, GA

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space. We work from stamped plans, coordinate rough-in with your general contractor and schedule inspections so the plumbing is never what holds up the opening date. Old lines get capped. We cut abandoned lines back to the main and cap them, so there are no dead legs or sewer gas problems later.

    Grease Trap Installation in Atlanta

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Atlanta, GA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.
