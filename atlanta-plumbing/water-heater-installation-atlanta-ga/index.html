    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Atlanta

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Atlanta, GA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Atlanta

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Georgia builds its plumbing rules on the International Plumbing Code with state amendments, and local building departments handle permits and inspections. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Atlanta, GA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Atlanta

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.
