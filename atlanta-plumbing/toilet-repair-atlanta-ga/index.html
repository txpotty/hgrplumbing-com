    Dependable Toilet Repair Services in Atlanta, GA

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Atlanta, GA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Atlanta

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Atlanta

    A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water annually, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage. Our technicians in Atlanta are experts at identifying the source of leaks, whether it's a cracked tank or bowl, a faulty wax ring, or a worn-out fill valve. We use specialized dyes and pressure tests to accurately locate hidden leaks and provide durable repairs, often involving the replacement of seals, gaskets, or the entire flushing mechanism. Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so prompt repair is crucial.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Atlanta

    A toilet that constantly runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in Atlanta is typically caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with high-quality, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop unnecessary water consumption.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Atlanta, GA

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Atlanta

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts quickly. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Atlanta

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Atlanta can lead to serious water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often require replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform careful tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Repair in Atlanta

    The cost of toilet repair in Atlanta, GA can vary a lot depending on the issue. Minor repairs like replacing a flapper might range from $100 to $250, while more complex issues such as a new fill valve or wax ring replacement could be $200 to $400. Severe clogs requiring professional augering might fall in the $150 to $350 range. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Swapping a flapper is a fair DIY job if you match the right size and the flush valve seat is smooth. Base leaks, flange repairs and cracked tanks are better left to a plumber because a bad reset can cause hidden water damage to the subfloor.

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium deposits on flush valve seats, fill valve ports and rim jets. Over time this causes leaks, slow refills and weak flushes. Descaling and choosing parts made to resist scale help repairs hold up longer.

    Most flapper, fill valve and supply line repairs take 20 to 45 minutes. Pulling and resetting a toilet typically takes about an hour, and flange repairs can add another 30 to 60 minutes.

    Our experienced plumbers are equipped to repair all major toilet brands commonly found in Atlanta, GA homes and businesses. This includes well-known manufacturers like Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, Eljer, Gerber, and more. We stock common parts for fast service.

    Weak flushing can be caused by several issues, including insufficient water in the tank, a clogged rim jet, a partially closed stop valve, or a partial clog in the drain line. Our plumbers can diagnose the exact cause and restore full flushing power.

    A failed wax ring or a loose, cracked flange is the most common cause. Condensation on the tank can also drip down and look like a base leak. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange and reset it on a fresh seal.

    While some minor running toilet issues, like a simple flapper adjustment, can be DIY, more complex problems may require a professional. If you're unsure about the cause, or if the issue persists after basic troubleshooting, calling HGR Plumbing ensures an accurate diagnosis and lasting repair, avoiding potential water waste or damage.
