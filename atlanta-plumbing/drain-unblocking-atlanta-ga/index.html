    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Unblocking in Atlanta

    The cost of drain unblocking in Atlanta, GA can vary a lot depending on the severity and location of the clog. Simple sink clogs might range from $150 to $300, while main sewer line blockages or those requiring hydro jetting could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide clear, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors from drains, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If you notice any of these symptoms, it's best to address them quickly to prevent a full blockage.

    The time it takes to unblock a drain depends on the nature and location of the clog. A simple sink or toilet clog might be cleared within an hour, while a main sewer line blockage requiring hydro jetting and camera work could take several hours. We always work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home or business.

    No permit is needed to clear a blocked drain. Permits only apply if repairs or replacements are required.

    For routine drain unblocking or simple repairs, a permit is typically not required. However, if the work involves major changes to your plumbing system, such as replacing a section of your main sewer line, a permit from Atlanta would likely be necessary. We handle all necessary permits when they apply.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets.     Stop using water in the affected fixture and connected drains. If a toilet is overflowing, turn off the supply valve behind it.
