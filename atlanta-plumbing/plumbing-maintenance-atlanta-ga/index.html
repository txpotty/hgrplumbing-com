    What a Plumbing Maintenance Visit Covers in Atlanta, GA

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Atlanta

    Sewer problems hide underground. A camera run from the cleanout shows root intrusion, grease buildup, sags and offset joints long before they cause a backup into the lowest drain in the house. Much of Georgia sits on heavy red clay that swells when wet and shrinks in drought, which can stress slab plumbing and sewer laterals over time. You see the footage. Catching a cracked joint early can mean a spot repair instead of a full replacement.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Atlanta, GA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Sediment comes out either way. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Atlanta, GA

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Northern and higher-elevation areas see more frozen-pipe calls than the south, where a true hard freeze may only come every few winters. We find exposed pipes, unprotected outdoor faucets and lines that need draining before the tough season. Pipe sleeves cost little. Burst pipes are not.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Atlanta

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Water Softener Service in Atlanta

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Atlanta, GA

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. The recording is yours. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Atlanta

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Municipal water across much of the northern half of Georgia comes from rivers and reservoirs and tends to run soft, which is gentle on fixtures and water heaters. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Atlanta, GA

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. Under-sink checks, toilet valve tests, fixture runs, washer hose inspection and a pressure reading cover the house. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Bigger items go on the report.
