    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Albany

    The cost of sewer cleaning in Albany can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method used, such as snaking or hydro jetting, and whether a camera inspection is required. More complex issues like heavy root intrusion or main line blockages will be at the higher end of this range.

    While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY solutions, main sewer line blockages require professional tools and expertise. Trying to clear a sewer line without proper equipment can worsen the problem or damage your pipes.

    Common signs include multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul odors coming from drains, and sewage backing up into sinks, tubs, or toilets. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Standard homeowners policies often exclude sewer backup unless you have a specific rider. Check your policy, and we can provide photos and notes for any claim.

    For standard sewer cleaning, such as snaking or hydro jetting to clear a blockage, a permit is usually not required in Albany.     Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to thoroughly clean the inside of your sewer pipes, removing grease, sludge, and roots. It is used for stubborn blockages, preventative maintenance, or when traditional snaking is not enough to restore full pipe diameter.
