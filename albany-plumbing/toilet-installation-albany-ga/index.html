    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Albany, GA

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Albany

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Albany, GA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Albany

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Albany, GA

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. Replacing a single 3.5 gallon toilet in a household of four can save several thousand gallons a year, and some water utilities have offered rebates on high-efficiency models, so it is worth asking yours. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Albany, GA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Albany

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Albany

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line. Powered seats with warm water and a dryer need a GFCI outlet within a few feet, and if one is not there, we line up a licensed electrician before we mount anything. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.
