    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Albany

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Albany

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Albany

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Albany, GA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations.
