    Reliable Sewer Replacement Services in Albany, GA

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Albany

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Albany homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Albany, GA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Albany, GA

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on later maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Albany

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Albany, GA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Albany, GA

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to prevent future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Albany

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Albany, GA

    Many older homes in Albany, GA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a major upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.
