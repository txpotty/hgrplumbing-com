    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Albany

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    HGR Plumbing stands by the quality of our work. We offer a warranty on our rooter services, the details of which will be provided with your estimate. This gives you confidence knowing that our solutions are designed to last.

    We use top-quality equipment for our rooter services. Our technicians commonly use professional-grade tools from brands like RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz for drain augers, sewer cameras, and hydro-jetting machines. These brands are known for their durability and performance in tough plumbing situations.

    Most standard rooter services for localized clogs can be completed in 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as main sewer line root removal or extensive hydro-jetting, may take longer, potentially 3 to 6 hours. We aim to complete the job promptly while staying thorough.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows signs of damage.

    Key signs include very slow drains, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, foul odors from drains, and water backing up in sinks, tubs, or toilets. If multiple fixtures are draining slowly, it often points to a main sewer line blockage requiring professional rooter service.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and usually more effective.
