    Electric Tank Repair in Albany, GA

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Hard water piles scale on the lower element until it burns out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Albany

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect. We test resistance with a meter. Elements and thermostats are on the truck, so the fix is usually done the same visit.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Albany

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. A descaling flush, a clean inlet screen and a check of the igniter and flow sensor cover most tankless calls. All major brands.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Albany

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check. Pilots that light and quit usually mean a weak thermocouple or a plugged arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.
