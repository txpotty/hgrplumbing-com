    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Albany

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Albany homes are better off with a backup system due to storm risks.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe, proper and effective installation.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are common signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also point to trouble.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a smart investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm typically runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.
