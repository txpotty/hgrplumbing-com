    Sump Pump Maintenance in Albany, GA: What We Check

    Float Switch Checks in Albany, GA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Albany, GA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Albany

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Albany, GA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Albany

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Albany

    Long dry spells also matter, because clay soil pulls away from pipes in drought and roots chase moisture into cracked sewer laterals. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Albany

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Albany, GA

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. Often it is just a tripped GFCI or a hung float. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.
