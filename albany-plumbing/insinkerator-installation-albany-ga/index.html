    New Construction Disposal Installs in Albany

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. During rough-in, the drain stub height gets set and the electrician leaves a switched outlet in the sink cabinet.     Badger Replacement for Albany Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. We pull the old unit first.     Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Albany, GA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Albany

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.
