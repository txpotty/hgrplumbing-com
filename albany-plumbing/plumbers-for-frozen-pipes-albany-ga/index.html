    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Albany, GA

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. Self-regulating cable on a GFCI outlet, covered with insulation, keeps the pipe above freezing.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Albany

    Foam pipe insulation is cheap and stops most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. It has to fit. Seams get taped and corners get mitered, because one bare fitting is often where the freeze starts.

    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection and Repair in Albany, GA

    A pipe that froze and thawed without leaking may still be damaged. A pressure test tells us. A gauge on the isolated line shows a hairline split within minutes.

    Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Albany, GA

    Water spraying from a wall or ceiling is a burst pipe. Close the main shutoff right away. Our plumber cuts out the damaged section, replaces it with new copper or PEX, and pressure tests the whole branch before turning water back on, since one freeze often splits a pipe in more than one place.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Albany

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Urgent Frozen Pipe Thawing in Albany

    Time matters once a line freezes, because the longer ice sits, the more pressure builds behind it. Crack open the faucet it serves. Most frozen lines flow again within an hour of arrival, and every one gets a pressure test.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Albany, GA

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a hose bib with the hose left on. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow devices sit fully exposed, so we drain or cover them before winter and repair split ones.
