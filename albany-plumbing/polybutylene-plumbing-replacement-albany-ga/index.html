    How We Replace Polybutylene in Albany, GA

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Albany

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Albany

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Albany, GA

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX is the cheaper option. A planned replacement usually costs less than the drywall, flooring and cabinet repairs after a single burst, and that is before any deductible or mold cleanup gets added on. Our estimate lists labor, materials, permit and patching separately.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Albany, GA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Permits for Polybutylene Replacement in Albany

    A whole-house poly replacement in Albany is a repipe, and it usually needs a plumbing permit. The permit is on us. The inspector looks at the new lines with the walls still open, checking supports, nail plates, shutoffs and the pressure test before anything is patched. That record helps later with insurance and resale.

    Copper Replacement Option in Albany

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. We solder or press each joint. Since copper is pricier and slower to install, we walk through cost, water conditions and your plans for the house before you decide.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Albany, GA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Albany

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. Plan on a higher price. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.
