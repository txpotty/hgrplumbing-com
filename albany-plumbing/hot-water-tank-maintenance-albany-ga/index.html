    Thorough Hot Water Tank Maintenance Services in Albany, GA

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Albany, GA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Albany, GA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and potentially leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Albany, GA, where mineral content in the water is a common concern.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Albany

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Albany, GA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Albany, GA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.
