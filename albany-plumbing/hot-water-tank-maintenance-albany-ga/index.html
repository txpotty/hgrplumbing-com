    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Albany, GA

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. On gas units we inspect the burner, thermocouple and vent connector. On electric units we meter both elements and check each thermostat terminal for scorching. Both get a flush.

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Albany

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. That alone takes 30 to 40 minutes. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips. Small leaks at the dielectric nipples get noted on your report.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Albany

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. We log every tank we service. The log shows the serial date, anode condition, the last relief valve test and a rough estimate of years left, so you can plan replacements during a vacancy instead of on a Saturday night. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Albany, GA

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. We pull the burner assembly and brush it clean. We also check the thermocouple or flame sensor, since a weak one lets the pilot go out every few days and leaves you resetting it on your knees. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Albany, GA

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Albany

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. On some models the anode sits under a plastic top cap. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. We carry the right sockets for each brand.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Albany

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. A tripped reset is a symptom. We find out why it tripped, which usually comes down to a shorted element or a thermostat stuck closed, before we press it back in. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Albany, GA

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it. We put in an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.
