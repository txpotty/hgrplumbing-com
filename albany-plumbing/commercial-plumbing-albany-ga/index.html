    Commercial Water Heater Service in Albany, GA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless racks get descaled. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Albany, GA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Running toilets count too. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Albany, GA

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Albany, GA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Albany

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away.
