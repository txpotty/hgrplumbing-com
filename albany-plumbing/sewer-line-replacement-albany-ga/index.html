    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Albany, GA

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Albany

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Albany

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Albany, GA

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rock adds time. The estimate accounts for depth and soil once we have located the line. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Albany

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Albany, GA

    Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes make most supply and drain repairs easier to reach. The usual suspects are rooted clay joints, cast iron worn through at the bottom and sections that sank as the soil settled. A new line keeps sewage from coming up through the basement floor drain or a ground floor shower.

    After-Hours Sewer Collapse Response in Albany

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is a health problem. Our on-call tech can clear a partial blockage, scope the line and tell you if the house can safely use its drains overnight or if everything needs to stay off until the repair. Mop-up waits until the line is sound. If drywall or carpet got wet, we can refer a restoration company.

    Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in Albany

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. The tech scopes the line, marks the break and lets you know whether the dig can start next day or has to wait on locates. Locates usually take a couple of business days. We can often set up a bypass so one bathroom stays usable.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Albany, GA

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.
