    Why Choose HGR Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Dalton, GA?

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Dalton

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring.     PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Dalton, GA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Dalton, GA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines.
