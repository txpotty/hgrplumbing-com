    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Dalton

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate.     Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Dalton, GA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Dalton

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Dalton

    Homes farther south often draw from deep limestone aquifers, so harder water and the occasional sulfur smell are common complaints there. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. Flow comes back. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Dalton

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Dalton

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Noritz Descaling in Dalton, GA

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.
