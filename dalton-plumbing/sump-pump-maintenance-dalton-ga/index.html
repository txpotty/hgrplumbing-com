    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Dalton

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Dalton

    Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain fast, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Dalton

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Dalton

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Dalton

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Dalton, GA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff. We clean their screens, test the floats and follow the discharge until it reaches daylight or an approved drain, because a line that dead-ends under the house just moves the water around. Northern and higher-elevation areas see more frozen-pipe calls than the south, where a true hard freeze may only come every few winters.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Dalton

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Dalton, GA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.
