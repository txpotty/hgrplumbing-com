    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Dalton, GA

    Grease Trap Installation in Dalton

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Dalton

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Dalton, GA

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Dalton, GA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless racks get descaled. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Dalton

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. We repair leaks, replace water heaters, clear stacks that serve several floors and repipe whole buildings while keeping residents informed about every shutoff. Managers get steady unit pricing. Each invoice shows the unit number so it can be charged to the right ledger.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Dalton, GA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Dalton

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Dalton

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.
