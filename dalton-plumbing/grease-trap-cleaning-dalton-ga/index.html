    Why Choose HGR Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Dalton, GA?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past about 25 percent full, the separation stops working. The grease then slips past the outlet into the lateral, cools into a hard white crust, and a cable machine only bores a narrow hole through it.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the sour, rotten-egg smell that decaying grease gives off.
    • System Longevity, Less grease in the line means fewer jetting calls and a longer life for the drain pipe.
    • Compliance Records, Each visit gets a dated record with gallons removed and the condition of the trap.

    Citywide Coverage

    Multi-unit restaurant groups can put every location on one account. You get one schedule and one invoice format. We stagger the pump-outs so each store gets a visit on its slowest morning, and the service sheets all go to one office.

    Experienced Team

    Techs ask the kitchen staff questions, because the cook knows more about what goes down the drain than anyone. Staff habits move the numbers. Scraping plates into the trash before they hit the dish machine can stretch a pumping interval by weeks, and our techs will point that out when they see it.

    Safety-First Approach

    We do not sell enzyme or bacteria additives as a replacement for pumping. Many districts ban that practice. The additives split grease into fine droplets that slip past the outlet and harden again downstream, the very thing the trap is there to prevent.
