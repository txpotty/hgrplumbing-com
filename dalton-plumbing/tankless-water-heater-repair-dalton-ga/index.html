    Recirculation Pump Repair in Dalton

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. When the pump fails or the check valve sticks, you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Dalton, GA

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, so the unit lights at low flow and fails when two showers run.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Dalton

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Dalton, GA

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Dalton

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, since pressure that sags under load leads to flame loss and slugs of cold water. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Dalton

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Dalton, GA

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Dalton

    Navien condensing units run a second stainless exchanger that pulls heat from the exhaust. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. On NPE-A models we also test the built-in recirculation pump, after clearing the trap and checking vent pitch. Then we fire it at low and high flow.
