    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Dalton

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Dalton

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Dalton

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Dalton, GA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Dalton

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Dalton, GA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Dalton

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Dalton

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.
