    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Dalton

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Preventative Maintenance Plans in Dalton, GA

    HGR Plumbing offers complete preventative maintenance plans for homeowners in Dalton, GA.     Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Dalton

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Dalton

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Dalton, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Emergency Water Heater Inspection in Dalton, GA

    If your water heater is acting up and you're unsure why, our emergency inspection service in Dalton, GA can diagnose the problem fast.     Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in Dalton, GA

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed. Regular flushing and anode replacement can help a tank reach the high end of its expected lifespan. Tankless units in particular can last many years with proper descaling. If your heater is near the end of its life or leaking from the tank, we will tell you honestly. We help you weigh repair against replacement with actual numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Dalton, GA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Dalton, GA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
