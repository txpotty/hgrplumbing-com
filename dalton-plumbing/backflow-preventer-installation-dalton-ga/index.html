    HGR Plumbing stands behind our work with a full warranty on our backflow preventer installations. This warranty covers both the labor and the installed device, providing you with peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be included with your quote.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, usually just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    Backflow preventers are usually installed as a preventive measure or because code requires them. Signs of possible cross-contamination include discolored or foul-smelling tap water, unexplained drops in water pressure or visible dirty water entering a clean water source, though these can also point to broader plumbing issues as well.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.

    In Dalton, most backflow preventers must be tested annually by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is crucial for ongoing compliance and safety.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, especially if piping must be reconfigured.
