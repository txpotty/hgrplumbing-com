    Compression faucets, often found in older Dalton homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Dalton

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight.     Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Dalton
