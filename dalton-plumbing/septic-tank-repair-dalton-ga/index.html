    Septic Pump and Alarm System Repair in Dalton

    Many modern septic systems, especially those on sloped properties or with advanced treatment units, depend on a working septic pump and alarm. When your pump fails or the alarm sounds, it indicates a serious issue. Our team can repair or replace faulty pumps, floats and electrical components so your system effectively moves wastewater to the drain field. We service all major pump brands for homes in Dalton.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Dalton

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Drain Field Repair and Restoration in Dalton, GA

    A failing drain field can cause standing water, lush green patches and sewage odors in your yard. HGR Plumbing specializes in diagnosing drain field problems, which often stem from compaction, hydraulic overload or biomat buildup. We offer complete repair solutions, including aeration, soil rejuvenation and partial or full drain field replacement, tailored to the soil conditions on your Dalton, GA property.

    Septic Pipe Repair and Replacement in Dalton, GA

    Damaged pipes leading to or from your septic tank can cause backups, leaks and unpleasant odors. These pipes, often made of PVC or cast iron, can crack due to shifting soil, root invasion or age. Homes are a mix of slab, crawlspace, and basement foundations, and crawlspace homes keep most supply and drain repairs easier to reach. HGR Plumbing offers full pipe repair and replacement services, including excavation, precise pipe fitting and backfilling, protecting the integrity of your entire septic system in Dalton, GA.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Dalton, GA

    The inlet and outlet baffles inside your septic tank are essential for separating solids from liquids and keeping them out of the drain field. Over time, these baffles can deteriorate, leading to system failure. HGR Plumbing provides professional repair and replacement of damaged septic tank baffles, using durable materials that hold up to the harsh conditions within the tank and protect your entire septic system in Dalton, GA.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Dalton

    A clogged drain field is a common cause of septic system backups and can be a serious issue for homeowners in Dalton. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future saturation.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Dalton, GA

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers set off high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage backs up.
