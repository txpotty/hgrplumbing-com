    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Dalton

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation usually point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to identify the problem. In Dalton, we repair or replace tanks to improve your well system's performance and extend pump life.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Dalton

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Dalton, GA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Heavy thunderstorms and the remnants of tropical systems can dump a lot of rain in a short time, overwhelming gutters, yard drains, and older sewer lines. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Dalton, GA

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HGR Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Dalton operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Jet Pump Repair in Dalton, GA

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Dalton home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.
