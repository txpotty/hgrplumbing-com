    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Dalton, GA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Dalton

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Dalton, GA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Dalton

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.
