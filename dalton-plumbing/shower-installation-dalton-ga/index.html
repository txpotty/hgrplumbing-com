    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Dalton, GA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Shower Pan Installation in Dalton

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. Prefab pans get a level check. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Dalton

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. Then the valve. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Shower Stall Installation in Dalton

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Dalton, GA

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Dalton

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. An electrician runs the circuit. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Dalton, GA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.
