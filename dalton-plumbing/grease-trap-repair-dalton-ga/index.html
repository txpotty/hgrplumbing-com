    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Dalton

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines.     Grease Trap Leak Repair in Dalton

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we recommend replacement.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Dalton

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Dalton, GA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup.
